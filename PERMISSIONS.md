# What You Can Do With This

**Plain language. Not a legal document** — the actual terms are in
[LICENSING-NOTES.md](LICENSING-NOTES.md). This page is here so you don't have to
read that one.

---

## The short version

> **Use it freely. Teach it freely. Just don't sell it, and don't take our name
> off it.**

---

## Yes — go ahead, no need to ask

- **Use any of this to run your own home.** Obviously. That's what it's for.
- **Print it.** Tape it inside a cabinet door. That's the intended use.
- **Give a copy to a friend**, your kid, your roommate, someone who just moved.
- **Teach from it.** A church small group, a campus ministry, an RA floor
  meeting, a youth program, a support group, a class.
- **Adapt it for your group.** Cut what doesn't apply, add what does, translate
  it, reformat it.
- **Quote it**, in an article, a newsletter, a sermon, a lesson plan.
- **Hand it out** at an orientation, a move-in day, a transition program.

**Two conditions, and they're easy:**

1. **Say where it came from.** "From Mr. Life Manager" and a link is plenty.
2. **If you change it, your version carries the same freedoms.** Don't lock down
   something you built from open material.

## No — please come talk to us first

- **Selling it**, or selling something built from it. Including a rebranded
  version.
- **Removing our name** and presenting it as your own work.
- **Charging for access to it** as a product, a course, or a paid membership.

**None of these are automatic refusals.** They're conversations. Which brings us
to the next part.

---

## The awkward middle — and what to do about it

Real life doesn't sort cleanly into "commercial" and "not."

- A church charges $10 for printed workbooks to cover the copying
- A university uses it in a course students pay tuition for
- A nonprofit runs a workshop with a paid facilitator
- A program bills a grant for materials

**We know these are unclear, and we'd rather you asked than guessed.**

The honest answer is that we're inclined to say yes to almost all of it. The
restriction exists to stop someone repackaging this and selling it as theirs —
not to stop a ministry from covering its printing costs.

**So ask.** If your use is close to the line, we can grant you specific
permission in writing, usually for free. It takes one email and it means you
never have to wonder.

---

## Why it's set up this way

We want this to travel. The whole premise is that these are things people
weren't taught — which doesn't get fixed by locking the material up.

But we also want it to stay **attached to where it came from.** Not out of ego:
if someone sees a checklist that helped them and wants more, they need to be able
to find us. The name traveling with the material *is* how this reaches the next
person.

So: free to use, never free to sell, and the name comes along.

---

## What's different about the paid products

Everything above covers the **free material** — the frameworks and the free
checklists and guides.

**Paid products work like any other book or course you buy.** They're yours to
use and to lend, not to redistribute or resell. If you want to use one with a
group, ask us — group and site licenses exist and they're not expensive.

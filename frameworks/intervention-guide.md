# Mr. Life Manager: Coach's Intervention Guide
*A Step-by-Step Field Manual for Conducting In-Person Household Resets*

Based on the live, recorded methods of Dave Webster ("Mr. Life Manager") on January 15, 2014, and pressure-tested against real-world executive dysfunction, fatigue, and physical constraints.

---

## 1. Posture & The Coach's Role

As a Mr. Life Manager coach, you are **not** an interior decorator, an organizer selling custom shelving, or a therapist digging into childhood roots. 

You are an **external executive-function prosthesis**:
* **100% Non-Judgmental:** A messy room is not a moral failing or character flaw; it is an unmade decision repeated over weeks. You never sigh, roll your eyes, or lecture.
* **Physically Directive:** You do not sit on a couch with a clipboard while the client cleans. You stand in the room, hand them trash bags, clear flat surfaces, and keep the physical momentum going.
* **Friction Eliminator:** The client’s brain is exhausted. You hold the boundary so they only have to make one binary 5-second decision at a time.
* **Pacing Guard:** You enforce strict time caps. A session never drags into an exhausting 4-hour death march.

---

## 2. The 6-Phase Intervention Structure

```
[Phase 1: Walk-In] ➔ [Phase 2: The Core Question] ➔ [Phase 3: Stage the Exit]
                                                               │
[Phase 6: Tomorrow's Clock] ◄─ [Phase 5: Visibility] ◄─ [Phase 4: 3-Pile Sprints]
```

---

### Phase 1: Walk-In & Warm Grounding (10 Minutes)
*Goal: Disarm client shame, assess physical layout, and establish a clear landing zone.*

1. **The Walk-Through:**
   * Walk in and let the client show you the problem area. 
   * Acknowledge the overwhelm immediately: *"Your place just got away from you because you've been working hard. Anyone's place gets like this when life gets busy. We're going to knock this out in small bites."*
2. **Establish the Landing Zone:**
   * Pick one flat surface (a kitchen counter, dining table, or bed). Clear it completely in under two minutes. 
   * This clean surface is your neutral staging ground.
3. **Stage Physical Supplies:**
   * Unpack contractor bags (black for trash, white or clear for donations).
   * Pull out a permanent black marker and blue painter's tape or labels.
   * Bring a box of nitrile gloves and a bottle of basic all-purpose spray.

---

### Phase 2: The Core Boundary Question (5 Minutes)
*Goal: Define the single legitimate purpose of the room.*

Ask Dave's foundational diagnostic question:
> **"What is this room actually for?"**

* **If it's a bedroom:** *"This room is for sleeping, resting, and getting dressed. That means power tools, car parts, and unopened Amazon returns do not live here."*
* **If it's a home office:** *"This desk is for today's active work. It is not an archive for 2021 receipts or old user manuals."*
* **If it's a living room:** *"This room is for sitting down and having friends over. It is not a holding station for laundry."*

Write the room's single purpose on an index card and tape it to the doorframe or wall.

---

### Phase 3: Stage the Exit & Clear the Runway (10 Minutes)
*Goal: Eliminate smells, stage trash for single-trip exit, and open physical pathways.*

1. **Triage Smells First:**
   * Tie off any kitchen or room trash containing food scraps.
   * **Stage the bag right inside the front door** (do not make ten separate trips to the dumpster during the session).
   * Crack open a window to circulate fresh air. Never spray perfume over warm trash.
2. **Clear the Floor Runway:**
   * Clear a direct walking path from the center of the room to the doorway and front exit. 
   * You cannot organize a room if you are tripping over shoes or boxes.

---

### Phase 4: The 3-Pile Sort in 12-Minute Sprints (45–60 Minutes)
*Goal: Sort every loose item without decision fatigue or nostalgic stalls.*

#### The Guardrails:
* **Run Three 12-Minute Sprints:** Set a timer. Work at high intensity for 12 minutes, then take a mandatory 2-minute breather with water.
* **The 3 Legitimate Piles:**
  1. **Throw Away:** Direct into a black contractor bag.
  2. **Give Away (Donations):** Direct into boxes or white bags.
  3. **Belongs Elsewhere:** Carry immediately to the room it belongs in.
  *(Keepers for this specific room stay where they are until Phase 5).*
* **Zero "Maybe" Pile:** A "decide later" pile guarantees clutter rebounds within 48 hours.

#### The 5-Second Decision Test:
Hold up an object and ask:
> **"Have you used this in the past 12 months?"**

* If **No:** It goes to give away or trash.
* If they hesitate (*"Well, I might need this if..."*): Coach script: *"If you have to invent a movie plot where you use this, you don't need it. Let it bless someone else."*

#### The Trunk Off-Ramp:
* As soon as donation bags are full, **carry them directly into the client's car trunk**.
* Loading donations into the car trunk counts as 100% complete for today. They are out of the living space immediately.

---

### Phase 5: Eye-Level Ergonomics & Visibility Setup (20–30 Minutes)
*Goal: Put keepers away so the client can navigate their room half-asleep.*

1. **Kill the Black Holes:**
   * Never store loose tools or everyday supplies in opaque cardboard boxes where they disappear.
   * Use **clear plastic storage bins** with bold front labels.
2. **Frequency of Use:**
   * **Daily essentials:** Stored at eye level and within arm's reach where the client's hand lands naturally.
   * **Occasional / Seasonal items:** Stored on the highest shelves or deep in closets.
3. **The "Do It in Your Sleep" Test:**
   * Ask the client: *"Tomorrow morning at 6:30 AM when you're half-asleep, can you reach for your keys, charger, or coffee mug without searching?"*
   * Every primary tool must have an exact, permanent home.

---

### Phase 6: Lock in Tomorrow's First Move & Final Exit (10 Minutes)
*Goal: Prevent clutter rebound, bind tomorrow's momentum to an exact clock, and leave the house reset.*

1. **Lock in Tomorrow's First Move (With a Clock on It):**
   * Do not write an open-ended to-do list.
   * Hand the client a 3x5 index card and a pen:
     > **"What is the very first move you will make tomorrow morning? Pick an exact time."**
   * Script: *"Write: '8:30 AM: Empty the dishwasher and hang work shirts.' Put this card right next to your coffee pot."*
2. **The Final Exit Trip:**
   * Grab the staged trash bags and bathroom bin from the front door.
   * Walk them to the dumpster together on your way out.
   * One single trip. The house is clear, the floor is open, and tomorrow is locked in.

## 3. Operational Traps & Coach Scripts

| Scenario / Trap | The Breakdown Risk | What the Coach Does & Says |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Client Nostalgia Freeze** | Client finds an old high school notebook or photo album and stops sorting for 25 minutes. | *"Set that right on the bed. We are not opening or reading anything during the sort. We'll do a special 5-minute memory pass at the very end."* |
| **Sunk-Cost Guilt** | *"I spent \$200 on that jacket / kitchen gadget three years ago."* | *"The money was spent three years ago. Right now, keeping it is charging you monthly rent in peace of mind and floor space. Let it go."* |
| **The "I'll Sell It Online" Trap** | Client wants to create a 4th pile for eBay / Facebook Marketplace. | *"Unless you actively run an eBay business and will photograph it tonight by 8:00 PM, an eBay pile is just clutter with an excuse. Put it in the donation bag."* |
| **Over-Ambitious Scope Creep** | Client suddenly says: *"Let's do the garage and the attic next!"* | *"No. The rule is one finished zone. Doing one zone completely beats half-starting three rooms and crashing. We stop right here."* |
| **Closed Donation Centers** | It's Sunday evening and Goodwill is closed. | Execute the Trunk Off-Ramp: *"Put the bags in the trunk of your car right now. Set a phone alert for Tuesday 5:30 PM on your way home from work to drop them off."* |

## 4. Materials Checklist for the Coach

Keep this kit in your car trunk for every in-person intervention:
- [ ] 1 roll heavy-duty 42-gallon black contractor bags
- [ ] 1 roll white tall kitchen trash bags
- [ ] 1 roll blue painter's tape (for labeling surfaces and shelves without residue)
- [ ] 2 thick black Sharpie markers
- [ ] Pack of 3x5 unruled index cards (for "Tomorrow's First Move")
- [ ] Box of nitrile disposable gloves
- [ ] 1 bottle unscented all-purpose surface cleaner
- [ ] 1 pack of 6 microfiber cloths
- [ ] 1 roll heavy-duty packing tape
- [ ] Smartphone with timer app ready for 12-minute sprints

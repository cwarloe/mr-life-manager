# Mr. Life Manager pitch script (2026-10-26 spoken draft)

Hi, I'm Charles Warloe. I built Mr. Life Manager for Tyler guys like, early twenties, first real job, first apartment, but never been explicitly taught how to run a home.
Picture this: he has guests arriving in an less than hour, or he just moved an has a new apartment full of boxes, or he's at a point where the kitchen has slid into chaos. He doesn't have a plan - what to do first, in what order, when to stop. So, in our first example, what should be an ordinary, fun evening with friends becomes another panic-clean. And most advice online either offers a complicated, expensive program or just give him tips that keeps him scrolling — but neither has given him any tangible results.

mrlifemanager.com; however, offers him free stand-alone, action-oriented checklists for specific situations, like six ordered tasks get you guest-ready in about 35 minutes. A 15-minute kitchen reset stops the slide. Once he sees results, he'll be offered paid guides across multiple life domains.
What makes it mr. life manager different is that I learned these principles from my mentor, Dave, the original Mr. Life Manager. The tone of the site is directive, never shaming, like "put down the phone, do this first". Everything is a series of action-oriented checklists and every page is designed to produce tangible results before asking for anything from users in return."

# Mr. Life Manager one-page summary

Practical life guides for young adults living on their own. Skills most were never taught and never knew they needed – until today..

Charles Warloe · hello@mrlifemanager.com · mrlifemanager.com

**The problem.** Most adults were never explicitly taught how to run a home. When guests are coming over, when they move into a first place, or when the household slides into chaos, they have no system — what to do first, in what order, when to stop. What should be an ordinary, fun evening with friends becomes a panic-clean. Or they give up and stop having people over altogether. Most online advice either shames them into action and quickly becomes an overwhelming system, or is engineered to keep them scrolling without ever taking action or seeing results.

**The customer.** Tyler, 23: first real job, first apartment, first time without a parent in the building. Competent at work, genuinely lost on all of it. He doesn't know and he wasn't taught — and his dad likely never thought he needed teaching, either because his mom handled it, his dad didn't know himself, or it had been 20 years since his dad thought about what he should do. Tyler is the right person: he got a win in an area he believed he needed help in, and he will use it. How much he'll pay, and whether he will, is a separate question.

**The product.** Free stand-alone, action-oriented checklists for specific situations — guests coming over, first night in a new place, a kitchen that's slid into chaos. Eventually, a complete guide for multiple domains. Every free page is one issue, with step-by-step directions and a clear end state.

**Business model.** Win before your wallet. The free page delivers the win first — pain, solution, done. Only then do we offer the next thing: deeper guides at $9–19 in the same domain. We don't charge for what costs us nothing to give, and we don't sell to people who won't use it. The flagship is the house-in-order system, which includes cleaning, organizing, dishes, and laundry.

**Differentiation.** The origin story is real. Charles learned from his mentor, Dave, the original Mr. Life Manager. But what sets it apart is that the guides deliver immediate results: each one is a stand-alone checklist that ends in a done thing, not another article to read. Directive without shaming — "put down the phone, do this first." A series of action-oriented checklists that run across multiple life domains — not a complicated system, but not just a list of tips. And a content generation engine underneath: Dave's principles can be applied to a new domain in minutes — a friend's office-cleanup request became a practical guide on a live page in half an hour.

**Stage & traction.** Pre-customer. Live site, complete content system, working email automation. Running a disciplined discovery sprint now: potential customer interviews and a feedback page with QR-coded flyers.

**What's next.** Prove demand: turn free wins into the first $9–19 customers, then earn the right to build more guides that sell and, ultimately, offer a more comprehensive series of guides as a flagship product.

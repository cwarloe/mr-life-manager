# Mr. Life Manager — 90-Second Pitch Script
~190 words. At ~140 words per minute this runs about 80–85 seconds. Time a spoken rehearsal; pauses count. Keep the final MP4 at or below 90 seconds.

---

Hi, I'm Charles Warloe. I built Mr. Life Manager for adults who were never explicitly taught how to run a home.

Picture it: guests arriving in an hour, or you're standing in a new apartment surrounded by boxes, or the kitchen has slid into chaos. Most people have no system for these moments — what to do first, in what order, when to stop. So a normal evening turns into panic-cleaning and stress. And most advice online is shaming, overwhelming, or designed to keep you scrolling.

Mr. Life Manager is a digital system: free task pages for specific situations, email guidance, and paid guides. Six ordered tasks get you guest-ready in about 35 minutes. A 15-minute kitchen reset stops the slide.

What makes it different: I learned this the expensive way, apprenticed to the real Mr. Life Manager, Dave Webster. The voice is directive, never shaming — put down the phone, do this first. One taught system for your whole life, and every page gives you something useful before asking for anything.

The site is live and the system is built. What's next is proving demand — I'm running a disciplined distribution test now, with a founding offer to find out who pays. Thank you.

---

**Delivery notes:** Direct to camera, clear sound, steady frame. Production polish is secondary to a clear customer, problem, product, and business model. The script is honest about the current stage — pre-customer, testing demand. Do not add claims about customers, revenue, or list size that you cannot substantiate.

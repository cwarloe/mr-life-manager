# Mr. Life Manager — One-Page Summary
### Practical life skills. One useful action at a time.
Charles Warloe · hello@mrlifemanager.com · mrlifemanager.com

**The problem.** Many adults were never explicitly taught how to run a home. When guests are coming, when they move into a first place, or when the household slides into chaos, they have no system — what to do first, in what order, when to stop. An ordinary evening becomes panic-cleaning and stress. Most online advice is shaming, overwhelming, or engineered to keep them scrolling.

**The customer.** Adults — especially young adults — in high-stakes home moments: a first apartment, guests arriving, life underwater.

**The product.** A digital system: free situational task pages (guests, first night, underwater), email guidance (The Week), and paid guides and checklists. Six ordered tasks get you guest-ready in about 35 minutes; a 15-minute kitchen reset stops the slide.

**Business model.** Free useful action before any signup → email list → founding offer and paid guides. A $39 First Place reservation tests willingness to pay.

**Differentiation.** The apprentice-and-mentor structure: Charles learned it the expensive way from the real Mr. Life Manager, Dave Webster. Directive without shaming — "put down the phone, do this first." One taught system across your whole life, not single-topic tips.

**Stage & traction.** Pre-customer. Live site, complete content system, working email automation, founding offer structured. Now running a disciplined distribution test: 8 Pinterest pins with a pre-committed continue/extend/retire decision rule, plus the paid-reservation demand test.

**What's next.** Prove demand: complete the distribution test, convert reservations, earn the first paying customers.

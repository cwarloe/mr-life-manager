## The idea (Charles, 2026-09-29)

Early-career, high-powered people: starting their own business, or in jobs with
long hours, heavy travel, or high intensity.

**This is the founder's own origin story.** Charles's notes record asking Dave
for "the full meal deal" to keep life from unraveling during a busy career
season, and Dave's advice to secure a separate, quiet space. That is a stronger
credential for this niche than any statistic.

## The competition is outsourcing, not other guides

People with money and no time buy help: cleaners, meal kits, laundry pickup, an
assistant. Spending money to buy time is linked to greater life satisfaction,
and a field experiment showed the effect is causal
([Whillans et al., *PNAS* 2017](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1706541114)).
So "here's how to clean your kitchen" loses to "hire a cleaner."

**What outsourcing can't do is the product.** A weekly cleaner can't handle the
mail, the paperwork, or remember when the filters are due. A meal kit doesn't
decide dinner the week it doesn't arrive. Help works only once someone has
decided what the system is: what to hand off, what to keep, and the ten-minute
nightly routine that makes a once-a-week cleaner enough.

> **Pitch:** decide it once, hand off what you can, and keep the small part
> nobody else can do.

It fits stewardship of time, and it makes outsourcing work instead of competing
with it.

## Three pains inside this niche

| Pain | What breaks | Product idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Long hours** | Nothing gets maintained; everything becomes a crisis | *The Floor:* the smallest week that still holds. *The Reset:* one weekend back to baseline (Dave's order: clean first, then maintain). |
| **Heavy travel** | The home decays while away; the mail stack and an empty fridge greet the return | **Travel Mode:** a pre-trip shutdown and a first-hour-home reset. A candidate **new free entry page** ("I leave Sunday" / "I just got back") — a real need tied to a date, the pattern [ADR-015](../../planning/decisions.md#adr-015--the-win-comes-before-the-email) wants. |
| **Founder intensity** | Decision fatigue; money and space blurring between business and home | **Decide Once:** standing defaults for meals, groceries and supplies on autopay. **Hand It Off:** what to outsource and how to brief a cleaner so it works. **One walkthrough session** as the premium tier for people with cash and no time. |

## Research on frequent business travelers

Searched 2026-09-29. There is solid research on frequent business travelers,
**almost none on single travelers specifically, and none found on how travelers
manage their homes.**

| Finding | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Preparation decides whether travel hurts or helps.** In a longitudinal study of 133 frequent business travelers, those low in *preventive coping* saw more trips lead to worse work-life balance, more emotional exhaustion, and lower relationship satisfaction. Those high in preventive coping saw more travel linked to *better* balance. This is Dave's preventive-maintenance idea ([ADR-006](../../planning/decisions.md#adr-006--preventative-management-is-a-core-framework)) in peer-reviewed form. **Caveat:** the study measured general planning ahead, not household systems. | [Niessen et al. 2018, *J. Organizational Behavior*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/job.2215) | High for the finding; Medium for our application |
| **Coming home is its own recognized stress point.** Traveler stress is studied in before, during, and after phases. After the trip, catch-up work and **household chores that piled up** add anxiety, irritability and fatigue. | [IESE](https://blog.iese.edu/expatriatus/2014/10/16/business-travelers-benefiting-the-business-while-risking-family-life/), [Chen 2019](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1467358417740747) | Medium |
| **The heaviest travelers are under real strain.** People away 21+ nights a month report more anxiety, depression, trouble sleeping, and alcohol dependence than those away 1–6 nights. | [Columbia, Rundle et al. 2018](https://www.publichealth.columbia.edu/news/feel-anxious-have-trouble-sleeping-you-may-be-traveling-business-too-often); [HBR summary](https://hbr.org/2018/05/just-how-bad-is-business-travel-for-your-health-heres-the-data) | High |
| **Singles are a large share of this group but aren't studied separately.** One road-warrior study's sample was 60% aged 25–35 and 41% single. The full paper couldn't be accessed to see whether singles differed. | [Travel well, road warriors (2017)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/312117418_Travel_well_road_warriors_Assessing_business_travelers'_stressors) | Medium |
| **Constant travel hollows out life at home** — less time for friends and community locally. | [Cohen & Gössling 2015](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0308518X15597124) | Medium–High |

**Inference, not research:** for a single traveler, nobody is home to take in
packages, empty the fridge, or deal with the mail, so the house just decays while
they're gone. Likely true; unmeasured. **The absence of research on single
travelers is also a market gap** — nobody is studying them, and nobody is
building for them.

## Implications

- **Travel Mode has evidence behind it.** Headline candidate: *"The trip isn't
  the problem. Coming back to a mess is."* A pre-trip shutdown and a
  first-hour-home reset are the preparation the study found makes the
  difference.
- **A values angle for single travelers.** The mission says homes should be
  "ready for the people in them." For someone whose local friendships are
  thinning, a home ready for guests is a way back into community, not just
  tidiness.
- **A possible institutional payer: employers.** Companies and corporate travel
  managers have a stake in traveler wellbeing, and travel-management companies
  already publish traveler-stress research. A licensed Travel Mode kit for heavy
  travelers is worth testing later. Unverified.

## Guardrails

- **Stay on the home.** Founders will pull toward business productivity —
  crowded, and not Dave's expertise. *We run the home so the business has room.*
- **Honest outsourcing advice.** No affiliate commission decides what gets
  recommended ([commerce rules](../business/commerce-rules.md)).
- **No health claims.** The anxiety and alcohol findings show real strain; health
  is not this brand's lane
  ([open decision](../../planning/decisions.md#open-decisions)).

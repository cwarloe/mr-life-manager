## Facts and source integrity

No verbatim intervention material was edited. These are our revised teaching
instructions. The audit remains a record of the earlier source snapshot; its
recommended changes are now implemented, not evidence of efficacy.

Food procedures use ready-cooked canned beans and package-directed rice and
vegetables. Egg and leftover guidance was checked against
[FDA egg safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety)
and [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety).
Lighting label guidance uses
[ENERGY STAR](https://www.energystar.gov/products/learn-about-brightness).
Laundry follows item and machine instructions; broad detergent doses,
care-label overrides, stain remedies and timing guarantees were removed.
This revision is not a comprehensive legal, medical or product-compatibility
review; uncertain specifics were removed or directed to the actual policy,
label, manufacturer or provider instead of guessed.

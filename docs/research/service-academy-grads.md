## The idea (Charles, 2026-09-29)

Service academy graduates have the money and the willpower, but have spent four
years in a highly structured environment. **Dave volunteers at the Air Force
Academy**, which makes this the one niche with the brand's own founder already
inside it.

## The twist: the opposite of the underwater professional

| | Underwater professional | Academy grad |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity and discipline | Low right now, no margin | High — selected and trained for it |
| What's missing | Time | **Systems they wrote themselves.** For four years the standard was set and inspected by someone else. |
| What they'll finish | The smallest thing that works in a bad week | A comprehensive manual |

The hook: *"You've passed a hundred inspections. Now you set the standard."*
It comes straight from Dave's own material — the 2009 weekly schedule says to do
an initial cleaning "to the standard that you desire"
([source](../../source-material/2009-11-04-dave-webster-weekly-schedule.md)),
and the 2010 house goals say "Have someone check you"
([source](../../source-material/2010-01-02-dave-webster-house-goals.md)).
Inspection, self-directed.

## Product ideas

1. **First Place, duty-station edition.** The comprehensive manual in their
   language: standards, routines, a weekly schedule ("battle rhythm"). The reader
   who finishes it.
2. **Set Your Own Standard.** A free, one-page worksheet: write your home's
   inspection standard and your weekly schedule. Built on Dave's material.
3. **The move-in kit for every new station.** "Up and running in a week" at each
   new assignment. They move every two to three years — the one **need that
   keeps coming back** anywhere in the ladder.
4. **A graduation gift.** Academy parents are organized and proud — the
   parent-as-payer from
   [ADR-010](../../planning/decisions.md#adr-010--beachhead-keep-the-audience-change-the-payer),
   with a fixed date on the calendar.

## Guardrails

- **No Academy name or logo** without the Academy's explicit agreement.
  Anything resembling an official endorsement could be a real problem for a
  volunteer. Dave recommending it personally is fine.
- **Ask Dave what his volunteer role allows** before using his Academy
  connection at all.
- Stay out of money advice beyond pointing to free installation counselors.

## Open questions for Dave

- What does his volunteer role allow?
- The exact vocabulary and calendar: when do seniors learn their first
  assignment, when do they move, what do they call things?
- Which cadets would he hand this to first, and would he?

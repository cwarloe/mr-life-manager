**Targeting (Charles, 2026-10-31):** these pages are for a person's first stretch
with nobody supervising them: no RA, no parents, no inspections, no structure
outside work. They are not for people who still live inside a supervised
system, such as junior enlisted in the barracks.

**Rules every line follows:** it names the situation, not the person. Times
match the pages: underwater ~90 min, guests ~1 hour, dinner ~10 min,
first-night ~90 min, one-room ~3 hours. "Three taps" is literal: the fork
button, then **Tonight**, then **Add to my calendar**. In the H1 lines below,
`<em>` marks the highlighted second clause.

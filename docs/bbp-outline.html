TITLE
The Manual Nobody Handed You
Practical adult-life systems for people who were never explicitly taught them.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role Point A
Adults are expected to run a home and a life, but most were never shown how. You're a capable person who learned by osmosis, or by failing until something stuck. Everything feels urgent because nothing was handled early, and embarrassment keeps people from asking.

How do we help without overwhelming?

• Which pain comes first?
• What is the smallest useful win?
• When is it right to ask for anything?		 Build it this way:

• Start with one entry page and one action

TITLE
The Manual Nobody Handed You
Practical adult-life systems for people who were never explicitly taught them.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
Adults are expected to run a home and a life, but most were never shown how. You're a capable person who learned by osmosis, or by failing until something stuck.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Everything feels urgent because nothing was handled early, and embarrassment keeps people from asking. Start with one entry page and one action, deliver a win, then invite the next step. A calmer life where things get handled early.

# Social launch kit

What already works well: [social-launch-kit.md](../../copy/social-launch-kit.md) measures the right thing. The conversion is a finished household job, not a click or a calendar save. It does not promote the still-unpacking draft, it does not reuse the retired $39 offer, and the company posts already avoid assuming guests come tonight. The Facebook and Instagram link plan is careful, and the file is honest that no posts or account checks were done by writing it.

## 1. The kit sets a different writing rule than the rest of the copy

Lines 4 to 5:

> Owner direction: clarity takes priority over complete sentences. Every element should move someone toward taking action and experiencing the result.

The repo's house rule is plain speech in complete sentences, with an object for every verb. This exception may be right for a small image card. As written, it also covers captions, the Facebook description, and the bio, and some of those lines get clipped as a result.

The Facebook description, lines 42 to 43:

> Company coming? Table covered? Place gotten out of hand? Choose the job you need to do. Follow a free checklist now, or put the task on your calendar for when you're home. No app or email required to use the checklist.

The first three questions are the clipped style Charles already rejected in other copy. The bio on lines 37 to 38 is closer to a complete sentence and can stay.

Suggested rewrite of the Facebook description, if complete sentences still apply off the image itself:

> Company coming, a table covered, or a place that has gotten out of hand? Choose the one job you need to do. Follow a free checklist now, or put that job on your calendar for when you are home. You do not need an app or an email address to use the checklist.

Whether image copy is allowed to drop objects and sentences is a direction question. See [needs-charles.md](needs-charles.md), question 16.

## 2. Preferred handles were flagged as free in September and are still unverified in November

Lines 31 to 33 say account ownership and handles remain unverified, and that the preferred handle is @mrlifemanager. The 16 September scan in [research-chores-2026-09-16.md](../../research/research-chores-2026-09-16.md) already recommended claiming that handle on TikTok, X, and YouTube as soon as someone was ready, and said Instagram and Facebook needed a manual check. Two months later, the launch kit still cannot say whether the handle is ours.

This needs a decision to go claim or check the accounts, or to wait. See [needs-charles.md](needs-charles.md), question 17.

## 3. The next steps are clear, and one public contact detail should stay consistent

Line 44 tells the operator to use charles@mrlifemanager.com. That matches the audience pages and the workflow finish-page offer. Some live pages, including the privacy page, still use hello@mrlifemanager.com. If both addresses work, the kit can say so in one line so a new operator does not think one of them is obsolete.

Suggested addition after the contact sentence:

> Use charles@mrlifemanager.com as the public contact on these accounts. The privacy page and a few other live pages still show hello@mrlifemanager.com. If both are read, leave them both. If only one is read, say which one in this kit.

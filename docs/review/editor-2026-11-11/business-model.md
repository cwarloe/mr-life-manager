# Business model

What already works well: the nine models are easy to compare, the membership warning is honest about fighting the brand promise, and licensing is correctly described as a long game rather than an MVP. The Dave-rights and faith-framing notes at the end point a writer to the right rules.

## 1. The opening says nothing is decided, then the sequence reads like a plan

Line 3:

> An exploration of how Mr. Life Manager could make money. Nothing here is decided.

Lines 206 to 217 then give a numbered "Recommended sequence" that starts with win-first entry pages, then $9 to $19 guides after a free win, then coaching, and cites ADR-015 and ADR-020 as the reason. A new reader will follow the sequence and miss the opening caveat, or will follow the caveat and not know which ADRs still govern.

Suggested rewrite of the opening:

> This is an exploration of how Mr. Life Manager could make money. The sequence at the end is a working recommendation, not a full set of product decisions. Where an accepted rule already applies, the model names it. Where a cited rule is on hold, the note says so.

## 2. The ebook price band does not match the current ladder

Lines 32 to 37 describe ebooks and digital guides, name the house-in-order system as the flagship under ADR-020, say deeper guides sit in the $9 to $19 band after a free win, and then give the economics as a price of $19 to $49.

Those two bands cannot both be the current rule. I am not suggesting a price. See [needs-charles.md](needs-charles.md), question 8.

## 3. The recommended funnel still treats ADR-015 as current

Lines 54 to 57 and lines 207 to 208 tell the reader to use the win-first funnel in ADR-015, including asking for email after the win. ADR-015 is on hold. The live site does work that way, so the description may still be accurate as a report of what the site does. As a recommendation for new work, it overstates how settled the rule is.

See [needs-charles.md](needs-charles.md), question 3. If the answer is that the live funnel stays as it is until the ADR is re-decided, a single sentence at the top of the recommended sequence would be enough:

> Step 1 describes what the site does today. The written rule behind it is on hold, so do not build a new funnel variation on it until that rule is re-decided.

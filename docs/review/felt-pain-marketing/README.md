# Felt-pain marketing audit

Owner correction, November 12, 2026. Audited main at `6946fde` before these notes. This is an adversarial editorial judgment, not audience testing. No videos, live task pages, PDFs, emails or social accounts were changed. Candidate lines below are proposals, not approved replacements. They describe an intended reader's situation; they are not interview quotes.

## Finding

The strongest entrances already exist in the workflow drafts: an actual visit tonight, dinner ready with nowhere to eat, and a move happening today. Several marketing adaptations remove the present difficulty, deadline or inability to start in favor of a general benefit. That is a loss of meaning, not merely a softer tone.

The standard is: **a specific present situation + a consequence they already care about + an obstacle the task actually helps them get past + one direct entry into doing.** A deadline can establish urgency, but ongoing distress and paralysis can also qualify. Do not invent a deadline, diagnose a viewer's psychology, or shame them. Not every person who sees an ad needs to qualify.

A person knowing how to do a chore is not the same as being able to choose a starting point while overwhelmed. Sell the guided way through today's problem, not the novelty of six familiar tips. Keep step previews out of this acquisition campaign. A useful result supports the promise after recognition; it does not replace recognition.

| `dinner.html`: “Dinner's ready. There's nowhere to eat.” | Best existing model: immediate event, tangible obstruction and wanted result. Preserve it. | **Dinner's ready. There's nowhere to eat.** | Make room at the table, clear seats and eat. Open the table task. No extra emotional language needed. | `one-room.html`: “There's a room you stopped using.” | Recognizable loss, but can be tolerated indefinitely; present need is missing. Name that bounded area in the offer. Otherwise hold this entrance: do not promise an entire usable guest bedroom from clearing one shelf. **Priority:** company tonight and overwhelmed/stuck reset first; preserve dinner as a strong smaller real need; first-night only for people moving now. Hold one-room as a leading campaign until its needed use and delivered zone align. This ranks present-problem fit, not measured market demand.

Candidate CTAs should name the job, for example “Open the link. Get your place ready for tonight.” Then the landing page offers immediate start or the exact time to do that job. Away from home is a circumstance to handle after recognition. It is not the main pain. For a tonight campaign the chosen preparation time must precede the visit; a generic next-week scheduling option does not solve tonight's problem. Current scheduler does not know the visitor's arrival time.

## Destination friction that stronger copy cannot conceal

The live pages place full scope/supply framing before Start and also offer reading/printing. That is necessary information mixed with a competing consumption path. Review on a phone: repeat the exact problem, name the bounded result, show start/schedule clearly, and stage required preparation at the point of doing. Do not auto-start before checking whether they are home, hide necessary supplies or remove safety information. This audit flags the issue; it does not claim a tested redesign or change the live experience.

The overwhelmed reset starts with the kitchen, which can be its longest job. Whether someone who feels paralyzed can begin that task is an execution question; a harder emotional hook cannot certify it. Keep the full finish honest. If the task fails people at that point, fix the execution path rather than advertising a partial action as the full win.

## Next copy pass

Start by drafting company-tonight and overwhelmed-reset entrances, with matching landing openings, before producing more assets. Review as an adversary: name why the intended person would keep scrolling or collect the advice, then address that failure. Do not approve because the piece is polished, polite or technically correct.

Ask a few relevant readers for recognition and observe whether they open/start, rather than soliciting abstract approval of the tips. Record what happens using existing task events and scorecard. Self-reported distress, time choices, calendar clicks and acknowledged finishes have different meanings; none establishes a permanent change in scrolling. No conversion forecasts or claims of validated emotional resonance in this audit.

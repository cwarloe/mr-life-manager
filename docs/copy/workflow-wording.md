## Decisions in this version

Charles hadn't answered some of these when this version was written. They are
recommendations, so reviewers can push back.

1. **"Still living out of boxes" is a checklist now.** Charles pointed out it
   can be built from the spare-room and first-night checklists, so workflow 6
   is a first draft of that. It's box 4 on the student and career fliers. The
   first-night wording moves to the move-in-season swap.
2. **Military keeps first night in box 1 and the spare room in box 4.** They
   overlap with workflow 6. A reviewer might say box 4 should be "still living
   out of boxes" instead.
3. **The checklist text is rewritten in plain speech** inside each workflow
   file. Same steps, same order, same safety lines.
4. **Defensive time wording stays out.** Charles removed the "this is a
   planning allowance" disclaimers on purpose. The times are still estimates,
   so each workflow file asks reviewers to time one themselves.
5. **Workflow 6 has two guesses in it:** the time (about two hours for one
   room) and one safety line about cutting boxes. Neither is in the source
   checklists.

## For reviewers

**What we want back:** line edits and gut reactions. "This sounds off" is
enough; you don't need to know why.

**How to read it:** out loud, as if you were saying it to a friend standing
next to you.

**Check each line against these:**
1. **Does every verb have its object?** No "pick a time" without what the time
   is for. No "it" or "this" that points at nothing.
2. **Would a person actually say this?** Mark anything stiff, clipped, or
   "clever."
3. **Is it about right now?** Cut anything about later, someday, or agreeing
   with a point.
4. **Is it true?** The flier lines have to match the checklist behind them.
   The times have to be believable.
5. **Is it kind?** It names the situation, never the person. Nothing should
   make a reader feel they failed.

**Questions we'd especially like answered:**
- **Military box 2, "Nobody inspects your place anymore, and it's gotten
  messy."** Too blunt, or about right for someone on their own for the first
  time?
- **Student box 3, "...you end up eating on your bed."** Is this true to
  students living on their own? Is the table the right problem, or is
  something else more common?
- **Career box 3, "...you end up eating standing at the counter."** Same
  question for people with long hours.
- **"Living on your own for the first time."** Does anything in the fliers make
  that feel like an insult?
- **The times.** Pick one checklist, do it, and time it. Tell us if the
  estimate is far off.

**Please don't spend time on** colors, fonts or layout. This round is wording
only.

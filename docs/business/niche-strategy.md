## The idea (Charles, 2026-09-29)

Go deliberately niche, and run several niches that are equally niche. Each gets
its own entry page and, where it helps, content framed in its own language.
Reasons:

- **Pages are cheap.** The site already runs on a shared entry-route catalog,
  shared templates, and `?from=` source tags. A new niche is mostly a new front
  page over the same content.
- **Targeted pages convert better** to the first step: the page names the
  reader's exact moment in their own words.
- **Word of mouth travels in tight communities.**
- **Help is tiered by need.** The knowledge is free to everyone. The few in each
  niche who need more help pay more, because more help costs more to deliver
  ([ADR-013](../../planning/decisions.md#adr-013--licensing-revised-free-to-use-never-free-to-sell)).

## Cautions

1. **The page is cheap; getting it in front of each niche is not.** Every niche
   needs someone inside it to pass the link along. The real limit on how many
   niches run at once is insiders, not pages. **Open a niche only when there is
   an insider.**
2. **No official institutional branding without explicit agreement.** Anything
   that looks like an institution endorsing us — its name, its logo — can cause
   real problems, especially for someone who volunteers there. Prefer *"for new
   lieutenants"* over *"USAFA edition."* A person recommending it is fine.
3. **Identical core, thin niche layer.** Change the headline, examples,
   vocabulary, and at most one niche-specific page. Never fork the core. Never
   charge one niche for something free in another — a fairness rule, and the
   ladder's "nothing sold twice" rule.
4. **Small niches produce small numbers.** No meaningful A/B tests at 40
   visitors. Judge each niche on a handful of completions, replies and
   conversations, using the existing `from=` tags.

## Five tests for choosing a niche

1. A **transition moment with a date**
2. A **tight network** where people talk
3. An **insider** Charles or Dave already knows
4. **Someone who can pay** — the person, a parent, or an institution
5. **Distinctive vocabulary** the page can match

professionals and Guard members, where Charles is the insider. Everything else

## Same content, two containers, matched to capacity

The niches split cleanly by how much capacity the reader has right now:

| | Low capacity (no margin) | High capacity (leaving structure) |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Underwater professional; high-intensity worker mid-crunch | Academy grad; anyone leaving a structured environment |
| What they'll finish | The smallest thing that still works in a bad week | A comprehensive manual |
| Container | *The Floor*, *The Reset*, *Travel Mode* | *First Place* |

This is a candidate answer to the open question of whether
[First Place is comprehensive or small](../../planning/decisions.md#open-decisions):
**both, for different readers.** Still to be decided.

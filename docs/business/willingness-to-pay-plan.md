# Plan: evidence of demand and willingness to pay

**Written:** 2026-11-05, in answer to the Bronco Entrepreneur Challenge 2026 judges.
**Status:** Draft. **Nothing in this plan has started.** Every step that contacts
an outside person or spends money is marked **Needs Charles's OK**.

**Goal.** Replace guesses in [customer-profile.md](customer-profile.md) with
evidence, in about three weeks, for $0.

---

## Part 1: interviews (5–10 people)

**Needs Charles's OK** before anyone is contacted.

**Who to talk to (recruit categories)**
- 3–5 people aged 18–25 who moved out in the last 12 months
- 3–5 parents of someone who moved out or graduated in the last 2 years
- People Charles already knows are fine for practice, but count their answers
  separately. Friends tend to be polite, and polite answers are weak evidence.

**Consent, said at the start**
> "I'm trying to understand how people learn to run a home. This takes about
> 20 minutes. I'll take notes but I won't record unless you say yes. I won't
> use your name anywhere. You can stop at any time."

Notes are stored without full names. No one is paid, and no one is sold anything.

**Rules for the interviewer** (based on the *Mom Test* approach, and on the
repo's existing [interview script](../research/interviews/customer-interview-script.md))
- Don't describe Mr. Life Manager until the end, and only if asked.
- Ask about what already happened, not what someone "would" do.
- Don't ask "Would you buy this?" The answer predicts very little.

**Questions for students and new renters**
1. Tell me about the last time your place needed a lot of work before someone
   came over. What did you do first?
2. When you moved out, what did you have to figure out on your own?
3. Where did you look for help? What did you find?
4. Have you ever paid for anything to help with this: a book, an app, a
   printable, a service? What was it, and what did it cost?
5. Did anyone give you something for moving out that you actually used?

**Questions for parents**
1. When your child moved out, what did you buy or give them?
2. Which of those things got used? How do you know?
3. Did you give anything to help them run the place, not just furnish it?
4. What did you spend on move-out or graduation gifts in total?
5. What would you not want a gift to say about your child?

**What we record for each interview:** past purchases and amounts, the moment
they described, the words they used, and anything they asked for without being
prompted.

## Part 2: an honest reserve page (price test)

**Needs Charles's OK** to publish, and to decide the price.

- A single page for a printed first-apartment gift version of the checklist.
  It shows a proposed price (for example, $15, to be chosen by Charles).
- One button: "Reserve one. No charge now." It collects an email only.
- The page says plainly: **"This isn't for sale yet. Nothing will be charged.
  We'll email you once if it becomes available, and you can decide then."**
- No countdown, no "only a few left," no fake scarcity. The email list is only
  for this one notice and is not added to the newsletter (same separation as
  the reminder group in PR #108).
- Traffic comes only from channels we already use (site, parents page).
  **Any paid ads need Charles's OK.**

A real preorder that takes money would be stronger evidence, but it needs a
product we can ship and a full refund policy. That is a later step and needs
Charles's OK.

## Success thresholds (decided now, before any results)

| Signal | Counts as evidence of willingness to pay if... |
|---|---|
| Interviews: past spending | At least 3 of the parents describe paying for a move-out or graduation gift that helped their child run the home, not just furnish it |
| Interviews: students | At least 2 of the students describe paying for help with running a home (book, app, printable) |
| Reserve page | At least 5% of parents-page visitors who see the offer reserve one, with at least 20 reservations total |

If the results fall below these numbers, we write that down as a result. We
don't move the line afterward.

## Rules this plan follows

- Win before wallet ([ADR-020](../../planning/adr-020-win-before-wallet.md)):
  the free checklists stay free; the reserve page is for a separate gift version.
- No shame, no pressure, no fake scarcity ([commerce-rules.md](commerce-rules.md)).
- ADR-010 stays on hold. This plan gathers evidence for that decision; it does
  not make it.

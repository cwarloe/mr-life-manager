## Has anything like this existed?

**Yes, in pieces. We found nothing that does all of it.**

- **Household systems for overwhelmed adults** have existed for more than 20
  years: FlyLady, Unf*ck Your Habitat, Dana K. White, Clutterbug. They sell well.
  They speak to people who already run a household.
- **"Adulting" for 18–25 year olds** has existed as books, courses and a
  storefront. The book sold well. Most courses show very small numbers. The best
  funded attempt, MassMutual's Society of Grownups, closed its storefront in
  October 2016 ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/business/2016/10/11/massmutual-closes-society-grownups-storefront-tries-reach-millennial-customers/tlWWzQgMnsffklO7tQBjcK/story.html)).
- **Move-in checklists** from Target and IKEA exist and are free. They are
  shopping lists. They tell you what to buy, not what to do.
- **Chore apps** (Tody, Sweepy, Home Routines) exist and are cheap. They track
  chores you already know how to do.

What we did not find: free, short checklists organized by **the moment a person
is in** ("people are coming over tonight," "the whole house needs doing"),
written in plain language, measured by **whether the steps got done**, and built
on a rule of no shame. That gap is real, but it is also small. Each piece of
Mr. Life Manager exists somewhere else. Our honest claim is the combination and
the voice, not a new category.

## What this means for the judges' question

1. **People pay for this subject.** Books (*Adulting*, *Decluttering at the Speed
   of Life*, *Unf*ck Your Habitat*) and Etsy printables show buyers exist. Prices
   seen are low: about $1 to $20.
2. **Selling directly to 18–25 year olds has gone badly** in every case we
   checked. This matches Judge 3's point that a parent is often the buyer.
3. **Our difference is real but narrow:** moment-based, plain language, done in
   minutes, measured by completion, and no shame. Some of the competitors above
   use shame or guilt as a motivator. We do not, and the judges named the voice
   as a strength.

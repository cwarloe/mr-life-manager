# Dave Rights and Compensation — Discussion Term Sheet

**Draft for discussion and professional review. Not an executed agreement and
not legal advice.** Keep the signed agreement and personal details outside this
public repository.

## Purpose

Record the understanding between Charles Warloe and Dave concerning the Mr. Life
Manager name, Dave's teaching, the recovered source documents, public
attribution, future recordings and compensation. The aim is to protect the
friendship, Dave and his family, and the continuity of the business by making
the existing understanding explicit.

## Parties and project

- **Project owner/operator:** Charles Warloe or the business entity later named
  in the final agreement.
- **Teacher/source contributor:** Dave [legal name in private agreement].
- **Project:** Mr. Life Manager and related free and paid educational material.

## Terms to settle

### 1. Existing source material

List the documents and recordings covered by the agreement. The private signed
schedule should identify each item by date and title. The repository's
[`source-material/INVENTORY.md`](../../source-material/INVENTORY.md) can be used
as the working inventory but is not itself the legal schedule.

Decide whether Dave grants:

- a broad, permanent license to reproduce, edit, adapt, publish, teach from and
  commercially use the material; or
- ownership of specified contributions to the business, with any retained
  rights stated expressly.

### 2. Future contributions

Define whether interviews, recordings, emails, photographs and new written
material are covered automatically or require a short written confirmation for
each session. State who owns the recording and transcript and whether Dave may
use them independently.

### 3. Name, story, quotations and likeness

Record separately what may be public:

- “Dave” only, or full legal name;
- the title “Mr. Life Manager” and the founding story;
- direct quotations and edited quotations;
- photographs, audio and video; and
- biographical facts.

Specify whether Dave reviews only factual accuracy or must approve each new use.
A narrow approval window prevents publication from being blocked by silence
while preserving his ability to correct something untrue.

### 4. Attribution and integrity

State the normal credit, how adaptations are distinguished from Dave's verbatim
words, and the right to remove his name from material he reasonably believes
misrepresents him. Verbatim source files remain unchanged in the repository;
interpretation belongs in separate files.

### 5. Compensation

Choose one structure and define the calculation plainly:

- fixed payment;
- percentage of specified product revenue;
- percentage after named direct costs;
- a hybrid; or
- another arrangement the parties understand.

Define payment timing, statements, refunds, taxes, audit questions, free
material, institutional licenses, bundles and products containing several
contributors' work. Do not leave “revenue” or “profit” undefined.

### 6. Free use and licensing

Confirm whether Dave's contributions may appear in free material distributed
under the project's intended noncommercial sharing terms. Clarify that the Mr.
Life Manager trademark and paid products are handled separately.

### 7. Ending participation

Address:

- Dave declining future sessions;
- continued use of already-published material;
- correction or withdrawal of inaccurate material;
- treatment of unsold and previously purchased products;
- use after death or incapacity; and
- whether rights and payment obligations pass to either party's estate or a
  successor business.

### 8. Business protections

The final agreement should also cover authority to grant the rights, third-party
material, confidentiality, independent tax responsibility, dispute handling,
governing law, amendments in writing and signatures.

## Completion evidence

The task is complete only when both parties have reviewed the same final text,
the agreement has been signed, each has a copy, and the private account-recovery
record says where that copy is stored. Do not commit the executed agreement to
this repository.

# ADR-020 — Win before wallet; retire the $39 flagship
Date: 2026-10-15
Status: Proposed

## Context
The $39 "First Place" comprehensive guide priced above verified category comparables and bundled everything into one complex offer — the opposite of the brand's single-issue, directive shape. (See competitor-notes.md: verified price points; $9–19 is the proven band for guides in this category.)

## Decision
- **Free tier:** single-issue task pages and basic checklists. One pain, one directive solution, one clear end state. Free forever.
- **Paid tier:** deeper $9–19 guides per domain, offered only after the free win has delivered real value.
- **Flagship:** the house-in-order system — cleaning, organizing, dishes, laundry. The physical work autopay can't do.
- **Principle:** we don't charge for what costs us nothing to make, and we don't sell to people who won't use it.
- **Subscription** only if action-gated: the next unlocks when the last is done. No dopamine without doing.

## Consequences
product-ladder.md, the First Place reservation page and demand test, and all $39 references need reworking. Dave's principles remain the cross-domain content engine (a friend's office-cleanup request became a live page in 30 minutes); the flagship stays in the home domain.

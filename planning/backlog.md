move at different speeds — content is the raw material, products are the
packaging.

**Priority key**
**P0** — do now, blocks the next phase
**P1** — do next, high value
**P2** — valuable, not urgent
**P3** — someday / needs a trigger

Each item is tagged with its [roadmap](roadmap.md) phase and primary
[domain](../frameworks/life-domains.md).

## Explicitly not doing

Worth writing down so these don't get reconsidered every few months:

- **A physical supply-kit business.** Inventory turns a high-margin business into
  a logistics business.
- **An app as the primary product.** Software maintenance would consume all
  available time and the audience's problem is not a lack of apps.
- **Professional organizing as a service.** Different business, crowded market,
  contradicts the teaching positioning.
- **Medical, legal, or financial advice.** We teach systems and cadences. Hard
  boundary.
- **Aspirational lifestyle content.** Wrong category, wrong audience, produces
  shame.
- **General life coaching.** Without practical systems it's the thing we're
  positioned against.

## How to work this backlog

- **Pull from the top.** If P0 items are open, nothing else moves.
- **One content item at a time, finished.** Half-written content has no value.
- **Content before products.** Products are assembled from content, not the
  reverse.
- **Every item should trace to a real problem** in
  [customer-problems.md](../docs/research/customer-problems.md). If it doesn't,
  ask why it's here.
- **Revisit monthly.** Move things down as readily as up. A backlog that only
  grows is a wish list.
- **Anything sitting at P2 for a year is really P3.** Be honest about it.

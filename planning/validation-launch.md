# Validation Launch

The current constraint is qualified use, not missing content. Run one route at
a time. Do not build another guide merely to create something to announce.

The `from` value follows a reader from the partner page to a free task, through
the completion page, and into the subject of a founding-reservation email. It
does not create a cookie, subscriber field, or visitor profile.

## Ready-to-send introductions

### Parent or family friend

I am testing a practical site for people running a home for the first time. It
does not ask them to study a course. You send the page that matches what is
happening today, and the task is already on it. If someone comes to mind, start
here: https://mrlifemanager.com/index-parents.html

### Church or young-adult leader

I am testing a free practical-life resource for young adults who were never
explicitly taught how to run a home. I am looking for a leader willing to try
one page with up to five people and report only what helped or where they
stopped—no names or personal details. The pilot is here:
https://mrlifemanager.com/partners.html?from=church

### Campus or residence-life contact

I am testing short, task-first pages for first-time independent adults. Each
starts with a real situation—first night, overwhelmed, guests coming, or a room
that became storage—and requires no account. I would value a five-person pilot,
not a program commitment: https://mrlifemanager.com/partners.html?from=campus

## Decision rule

After three completed five-person pilots, review the aggregate reports,
completion-path visits, replies, and founding reservations. Build the smallest
missing step that appears in at least two independent reports. If pilots do not
start, repair the outreach or audience fit; do not expand the product.

### 1. Who is this for?

Not recorded. The page assumes no prior knowledge ("Laundry isn't hard. It's
just never explained").

### 2. What problem does it solve?

The [Laundry](../../../docs/research/customer-problems.md#laundry) list almost
line for line: sorting, settings, detergent overdosing, stains, care labels,
lint trap, cadence, and the laundry chair. Listed as MVP priority 5 there.

### 3. What outcome does it produce?

Not recorded. The page's own promise: *"Normal, cold, and don't overfill"*
handles most laundry; the rest is refinement.

### 4. Which domains?

Home ([life-domains.md](../../../frameworks/life-domains.md)).

### 5. Which principles?

Not recorded.

### 6. Where on the ladder?

Free guide, archive and search surface per ADR-015, not an offer. The
[product ladder](../../../docs/business/product-ladder.md) and
[ebooks README](../../ebooks/README.md) also list a paid *Laundry, Solved* at
Rung 1; how that relates to this free guide is not decided.

### 7. What content?

Nine sections: sorting (three piles), dial settings, detergent dose, stains,
care labels, the dryer (lint-trap warning in amber), the laundry chair, the
rhythm (good enough / better), laundromats. No source document cited.

### 8. Price?

Free. Footer: free to use, copy and teach from with credit, not for resale.

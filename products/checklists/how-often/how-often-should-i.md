# How Often Should I…?

**The cadences nobody tells you, in one place.**

This is the most common question in running a home, and it's almost never
answered plainly. So: here's the honest version.

**Two numbers per row.** *Good enough* is the standard that actually keeps a home
working. *Better* is the ideal, for when you have the time or the situation calls
for it. **Good enough is genuinely fine.** A schedule you keep beats a stricter
one you abandon.

Organized by how often, not by room — so it turns into a routine instead of a
reference you never open.

---

## Every day (about 10 minutes)

| Task | Good enough | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Dishes — washed or in the dishwasher | Before bed | After each meal |
| Kitchen counters wiped | Before bed | As you cook |
| Stovetop wiped | While still warm | After each use |
| Put things back where they live | Once, before bed | As you go |
| Make the bed | Most days | Daily |
| Dryer **lint trap** | **Every load** ⚠️ | Every load |
| Food put away, nothing left out | Before bed | Right after eating |

> **The ten-minute closing shift is the highest-leverage habit in this
> document.** A kitchen reset nightly never needs a rescue. Skip it for four days
> and you've got a project.
>
> ⚠️ **Lint is a fire risk.** This one isn't about tidiness.

## Every week

| Task | Good enough | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Bathroom — toilet, sink, mirror | Weekly | Twice weekly |
| Shower/tub | Every 1–2 weeks | Weekly |
| Vacuum main areas | Weekly | Twice weekly, or daily with pets |
| Mop hard floors | Every 2 weeks | Weekly |
| Dust surfaces | Every 2 weeks | Weekly |
| Change sheets | Every 2 weeks | **Weekly** — the standard recommendation |
| Trash and recycling out | On your collection day | — |
| Clear out the fridge | Before you shop | — |
| Laundry | Weekly, set day | — |
| Wipe down the microwave | Weekly | When it splatters |
| Check bank accounts | Weekly, 5 minutes | — |
| Plan the week / look at the calendar | Weekly, 20–30 min | — |

> **Pick one day and stop deciding.** The deciding costs more than the doing.

## Every 3–6 months

| Task | Notes |
|---|---|
| **HVAC filter — change** | Every 1–3 months (90 days is the standard for a 1-inch filter). **With pets, every 30–60 days.** Check it monthly and change it when it looks dirty. The size is printed on the old one — check before buying. |
| Baseboards and door frames | Quarterly |
| Windows, inside | Quarterly |
| Light fixtures and ceiling fans | Quarterly |
| Refrigerator coils — vacuum | Twice a year. Improves efficiency and lifespan. |
| Flip or rotate the mattress | Every 6 months (check the manufacturer — some shouldn't be flipped) |
| Wash comforter or duvet | Every 3–6 months |
| Deep clean one room at a time | Rotate quarterly — never all at once |
| Review subscriptions | Quarterly. Cancel what you forgot you had. |
| **Verify your backups actually restore** | Quarterly ⚠️ — the step everyone skips |
| Dentist | Every 6 months, or as your dentist advises |

## Once a year

| Task | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Dryer vent duct — clean** | Annually ⚠️ **Fire risk.** Not the lint trap — the duct behind the dryer. |
| **Smoke/CO detector batteries** | Annually at minimum — **NFPA recommends every 6 months** for standard batteries. Pick a memorable date and keep it. |
| **Replace the whole smoke/CO alarm** | **Every 10 years, no exceptions** ⚠️ Sensors degrade even when the test button still beeps. The manufacture date is stamped on the back — go look at yours tonight. Required by NFPA 72. |
| Water heater — flush | Annually. Renting? This is your landlord's job — report it. |
| Gutters | Twice a year, if they're yours |
| Caulk and grout — inspect | Annually. Re-seal before water gets behind it. |
| Check for leaks under every sink | Annually — takes five minutes, catches expensive problems |
| Insurance review — renter's, auto, health | Annually, at renewal |
| Credit report check | Annually. Free from the official federal source. |
| Gather tax documents | January |
| Physical / annual checkup | Annually, or as your provider advises |
| Eye exam | Every 1–2 years |
| Wardrobe clear-out | Annually |
| Deep clean the whole kitchen | Annually |
| Review this list against your actual life | Annually |

## Vehicles — check your manual first

Intervals vary enormously by vehicle, oil type, and how you drive, so treat these
as "go look it up," not as the answer.

| Task | Typical guidance |
|---|---|
| Oil change | **Follow your owner's manual.** Modern engines and synthetic oils vary widely — the old 3,000-mile rule is usually obsolete. |
| Tire pressure | Monthly, and before long trips. Check when tires are cold. |
| Tire rotation | Per manual, commonly every other oil change |
| Registration and inspection | Per your state. **Put the expiration on a calendar.** |
| Wiper blades | Every 6–12 months, or when they streak |
| Air filter, brakes, fluids | Per manual; have them checked at each service |

## When something says "as needed," here's what that means

| Situation | Do it now |
|---|---|
| A spill | Immediately. Wet is 10 seconds; dried is 3 minutes. |
| A stain on clothing | Immediately. Set stains are usually permanent. |
| A slow drip or running toilet | This week. It's a cheap fix and an expensive flood. |
| A stain appearing on a ceiling | **Today.** Report it in writing if you rent. |
| Smoke detector chirping | Today. It means the battery is dying. |
| Anything your landlord is responsible for | Report in writing, immediately. The paper trail protects your deposit. |

## The honest footnotes

**These are starting points, not laws.** Pets, allergies, kids, a dusty climate,
working from home, or a health condition all shift them. Adjust and don't feel
bad about it.

**If you're behind on all of it, don't start here.** Pick one room — usually the
kitchen — get it to baseline, and add the ten-minute nightly reset. Add
everything else one line at a time. Trying to adopt this whole page at once is
the most reliable way to abandon it by Thursday.

**Renting?** A good deal of the annual list is your landlord's responsibility.
Your job is to **notice and report in writing**, promptly. That's the whole task,
and it's what protects your deposit.

**Health rows are scheduling reminders only** — your doctor and dentist set your
actual intervals.

**The marked ⚠️ rows are different in kind.** Dryer lint, dryer vents, smoke and
CO alarms, and backups aren't about a home looking nice. Everything else on this
page can slip a week without consequence. Those can't.

**Where the safety numbers come from.** The dryer and alarm intervals follow
National Fire Protection Association guidance: clean the lint filter every load,
have the dryer vent cleaned annually, test alarms monthly, and **replace alarms
every 10 years from the manufacture date** (NFPA 72) regardless of whether they
still chirp when tested. Filter intervals follow manufacturer guidance for
standard 1-inch filters. Everything else here is ordinary practice, not code.

**The 10-year alarm rule is the one almost nobody knows.** Sensors degrade
whether or not the test button works. Go look at the date stamped on the back of
yours — a lot of people find one older than they expected.

# The Cleaning Supply Starter List

**What to actually buy, what to skip, and roughly what it costs.**

The cleaning aisle is designed to make you think you need forty products. You
need about nine. Most specialty cleaners are the same three ingredients with a
different label and a different smell.

Here's the honest list.

---

## Buy these nine (about $40–60 total)

| What | Roughly | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **All-purpose cleaner** | $4–6 | Counters, tables, appliances, most surfaces. Your workhorse. |
| **Dish soap** | $3–5 | Dishes, and it doubles as a decent spot cleaner |
| **Bathroom cleaner** | $4–6 | Soap scum and mildew need something stronger |
| **Toilet bowl cleaner** | $3–5 | Plus a brush — $5 |
| **Glass cleaner** | $3–5 | Mirrors and windows. Optional; a damp microfiber does most of it. |
| **Baking soda** | $1–2 | Scrubbing without scratching. Absurdly useful. |
| **White vinegar** | $3–4 | Descaling, hard water, showerheads, coffee maker |
| **Microfiber cloths (6–12)** | $10–15 | **The single best purchase on this list.** Replaces most paper towels. |
| **Sponges or scrub pads** | $3–5 | Replace every 1–2 weeks |

## Skip these — at least at first

| Skip | Because |
|---|---|
| **Separate cleaners for every room** | Kitchen, living room, and bedroom surfaces are the same surfaces. One all-purpose does it. |
| **Wood-specific, granite-specific, stainless-specific sprays** | Buy these only when you actually have that surface *and* the general one isn't working. |
| **Disinfecting wipes as your main cleaner** | Convenient, expensive, wasteful, and worse at actual cleaning. Fine to keep a tub for quick jobs. |
| **Scented everything** | You're paying for fragrance. It also masks the smell of a surface that isn't clean. |
| **"Natural" premium brands** | Often the same actives at triple the price. Vinegar and baking soda are the actual budget natural option. |
| **Steam mops, robot vacuums, specialty gadgets** | Maybe later. Not in month one. |
| **Bulk anything, at first** | Buy small until you know what you'll use. Bulk cleaner you hate is worse than no cleaner. |

---

## One safety rule

> **⚠️ Don't mix cleaning products.** Some combinations — bleach with anything,
> especially — make fumes that will genuinely hurt you.

**One product at a time. Rinse in between.** If a surface needs two treatments,
rinse with water first. Open a window when you're using anything strong.

That's the whole rule. You don't need to know which combinations are the bad
ones if you never mix any of them.

---

## Where to keep it

**Not all in one closet.** This is the part that changes whether cleaning
actually happens.

Keep a small set **in each room where it gets used**:

- **Bathroom** — bathroom cleaner, toilet cleaner, brush, a couple of cloths
- **Kitchen** — all-purpose, dish soap, cloths, sponges
- **Central closet** — vacuum, mop, broom, backups, laundry supplies

Buying a second $5 bottle of bathroom cleaner feels wasteful and isn't. **The
bathroom gets wiped when the supplies are already in the bathroom.** Every trip
down the hall is a reason not to bother, and reasons not to bother are what you
are actually fighting.

---

## The upgrade order, if you want more later

Once the basics are working, in this order:

1. **More microfiber cloths.** Genuinely, more. You'll never regret it.
2. A **squeegee** for the shower — thirty seconds after showering prevents most
   soap scum, which is the worst job in the bathroom.
3. A **handheld vacuum** for quick messes
4. **Extendable duster** for ceiling fans and high corners
5. A **better vacuum**, once you know what you actually need

---

## If money is tight

You can clean an entire apartment with four things, for about $15:

**Dish soap. Baking soda. White vinegar. A stack of microfiber cloths.**

That covers dishes, counters, most surfaces, glass, scrubbing, and descaling.
Add a toilet brush and a plunger and you're genuinely fine. Everything else on
this page is convenience.

---

*Nobody teaches this, so most people learn it by buying $200 of cleaning
products and using four of them.*

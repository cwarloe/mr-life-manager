# I have to clear the table before we can eat.

Get your table back for meals, and give the things that keep covering it somewhere to go.

**Today's job:** Work on the dining table and the chairs needed for the people who normally eat there. Keep the rest of the room outside this job. The finish is sitting down to a meal without first relocating a pile.

**Have ready:** A trash bag, a cleaning cloth, and a small box to carry things back to their homes. Use a scrap of paper for anything that needs a later action.

- [ ] **1. Make a small place to start.** Remove obvious trash and take dishes to the kitchen. Clear enough of the tabletop to sort a small batch. Keep the route around the table open; putting the pile on the floor creates another obstacle.

- [ ] **2. Sort what is left by its next destination.** Work across the table in small batches. Separate things to throw away, give away, use regularly, and use occasionally. Ask before making decisions about someone else's belongings.

- [ ] **3. Put things with homes back in them.** Return the tools, clothes, bags, and other belongings that already have a place. Use the box to carry a batch, then empty it into those homes. Do not leave the box as the table's new storage system.

- [ ] **4. Give the repeat offenders a home.** Look at what keeps landing here because it has nowhere better to go. Put everyday items near where they are used: keys by the exit, work supplies in the work area. Keep papers needing action together in one visible tray and write a specific time to deal with them.

- [ ] **5. Move occasional-use things out of the working space.** Group the things you want to keep but seldom use and assign them a reachable storage spot away from the tabletop and eating chairs. Make the contents visible or label the container. Write down any missing storage and a temporary home that keeps the table usable.

- [ ] **6. Wipe the table and free the seats.** Lift the items that genuinely belong on the table, wipe underneath, and put them back only if the meal still fits. Clear the seats and enough room to pull them out. The table's useful space includes the people around it.

- [ ] **7. Complete the exits.** Take the trash out and finish returning the carry box. Choose a recipient or destination for giveaways, with a specific next-action time. If something still needs another person's decision, agree where it will wait and when to ask; keep the eating places clear.

- [ ] **8. Set the table for the people who use it.** Put out the places needed for an ordinary meal and sit down. If anything must move first, give it a home now. Leave the carry box empty. The job ends with a table ready to use, not a photograph of an empty surface.

**Your finished win:** The people who normally eat here can sit down to a meal without clearing the table first. The objects that usually block it have named destinations.

**Keep the result:** After using the table for another activity, return those materials to their homes. If the same object keeps landing here, make its home easier to use.

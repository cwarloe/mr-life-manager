# Product brief — Reset Your Space

**Status:** Paused Markdown draft, revised after completion audit on 2026-10-28. Not reader-tested; no measured completion or timing claim.
**Audit:** [Paused checklist review](../../../docs/research/paused-checklist-completion-audit.md).

## Audience, problem, and outcome

For Maya setting up an independent home and Dana trying to regain control of an
existing one ([audiences](../../../docs/vision/target-audiences.md)). This serves
the [home and organization problems](../../../docs/research/customer-problems.md):
clutter, undefined homes, and difficulty knowing where to start.

The immediate outcome is one usable area with visible, reachable, repeatable
homes. It serves the [cleaner, more functional homes outcome](../../../docs/vision/customer-outcomes.md#cleaner-more-functional-homes),
especially the target statement that daily items are within reach of where they
are used. That statement is a desired outcome, not a testimonial.

## Domain and principles

Primary domain: Home. Secondary: Hospitality ([domain map](../../../frameworks/life-domains.md)).
Uses [house principles](../../../docs/vision/principles.md) 1 (a defined home),
4 (systems over memory), 5 (easy access), 8 (dignity), 9 (simple enough to keep),
and 10 (explain why).

## Scope, format, and price

A free standalone checklist: one chosen area, twelve ordered actions. It covers
the full organizing method in the recording, rather than turning every object
mentioned into a separate chore. Repeat it for another area after this one is
usable. No promised duration; the recording ends with supplies still needed.

This is a Markdown draft in the repo, with no new public site route or signup
sequence. It can precede a facilitated session or support someone doing the
work independently. It is free under ADR-020 and ADR-022 in the
[decision log](../../../planning/decisions.md); no new paid offer is implied.
It adds the physical sorting-and-placement work to the existing
[room-purpose guide](../room-for/brief.md), which chiefly identifies a room's
job and next actions.

Bounding the work to a named complete area, labels as an alternative to clear
containers, completing trash removal, and a final usability check are our
adaptations. The recording shows trash staged by the door, not its final
removal. It does not establish a maintenance cadence or a lasting outcome.
The checklist's return-after-use instruction comes from 17:26–17:51; it does
not add an invented nightly routine. No domain-specific safety or food-storage
advice is inferred from the noisy conversational passages.

## Validation still needed

Have a reader use the twelve actions on one real area. Check whether the four
groups make sense, the area is left usable, and the chosen homes are easy to
use afterward. Storage purchases and scheduled handoffs remain unfinished
until they actually happen.

## Completion-audit revisions

The twelve actions remain. Before starting, the reader defines the complete area,
checks capacity, staging, access and permission, and names a practical use test.
Grouping stays within the boundary. Protected review items and pending handoffs
have labeled waiting places, owners and times. A usable area is distinguished
from actions that remain unfinished. These are our design revisions, not
additional transcript claims. No public route or publication flag changed.

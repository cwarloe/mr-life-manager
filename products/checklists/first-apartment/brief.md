# Product Brief — First Apartment Checklist

**Status:** Draft 1, 2026-09-08. Not reviewed, not designed, not tested.
**Tier:** Rung 0 — free ([product-ladder.md](../../../docs/business/product-ladder.md))
**Format:** 4-page printable PDF, black-and-white, US Letter

---

### 1. Who is this for?

**Maya** (first apartment after dorm life) and **Tyler** (leaving the parents'
home) from [target-audiences.md](../../../docs/vision/target-audiences.md).
Written so a **parent buying it as a gift** can hand it over without it feeling
condescending — a channel [revenue-ideas.md](../../../docs/business/revenue-ideas.md)
flags as potentially decisive.

### 2. What problem does it solve?

From [customer-problems.md](../../../docs/research/customer-problems.md):
"Doesn't know what a home actually needs to function — arrives with dishes but
no trash bags, plunger, batteries, light bulbs"; "never reads the lease";
"doesn't know where the water shutoff or breaker panel is"; "doesn't know what's
the landlord's problem versus their own."

### 3. What outcome should it produce?

Rung 1 (less chaos, less decision fatigue) and rung 3 (fewer crises) of
[customer-outcomes.md](../../../docs/vision/customer-outcomes.md). The target
quote:

> "I didn't have to figure out what I was forgetting. It was already on the list."

### 4. Domains covered

Home (primary) · Food · Money · Paperwork · Maintenance · Hospitality (light)

### 5. Principles it serves

§1 everything has its place · §3 preventative over crisis · §5 make the right
action easy · §8 dignity, not shame · §10 teach the why

### 6. Ladder position

**Nothing before it — this is the front door.** After it: the room-by-room
guides ($9) and *First Place* ($49). It must be complete and genuinely useful
with **no upsell in the body**; a free asset that is really a sales pitch
poisons the ladder, especially for an audience whose core barrier is
embarrassment.

### 7. Content drawn on

None — `content/` is still empty. This draft is **source material for
`content/`, not the other way round.** Sections here should be extracted into
`content/home/organization/`, `content/home/cleaning/`, and
`content/life-transitions/` once proven.

### 8. Price, and why

**$0.** Its job is to prove the audience exists and to build a list
([roadmap.md](../../../planning/roadmap.md) Phase 2), not to earn.

## Design decisions, and the reasoning

**Sequenced by time, not by category.** Nearly every competing "first apartment
checklist" is a shopping list. The research found the differentiator is a
**taught system**, not a list of things
([competitor-notes.md](../../../docs/research/competitor-notes.md#findings)) —
so this is ordered *before you sign → before you move in → first day → first
week → first month*. Sequence is the value.

**Deposit protection is the hook.** Verified in research: a *Safe and Sound*
reviewer wanted "how to fix shit but still get your security deposit back," and
a Rent Smart participant said, "Before I would just do one walk thru of how the
apartment look then just accept it." The move-in documentation gap is real,
costs real money, and almost nobody covers it.

**Prices are ranges, and deliberately undated.** Anything precise goes stale and
makes the whole document look wrong. Ranges age gracefully.

**No faith content.** [ADR-009](../../../planning/decisions.md).

**No first-person narrator.** The persona question is still open; nothing here
should presume its answer.

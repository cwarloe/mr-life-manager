# Worksheets

Fill-in templates — agreements, calendars, planners, and inventories. Things the
customer completes rather than reads.

## Why worksheets are strategically valuable

A worksheet produces **the customer's own system**, not ours. That's the brand
promise made literal: we don't do it for you, we teach you to run it.

They also have unusually high completion and perceived value relative to
production cost, and they're the easiest format to license to institutions —
a facilitator can run a session around a worksheet.

## Standards

- **Fillable on paper and on screen.** Both matter.
- **Self-explanatory.** Assume no instructions were read.
- **Include a filled example.** The single highest-impact addition to any
  worksheet — people need to see what "done" looks like.
- **Leave room to write.** Cramped worksheets don't get used.
- **Revisitable.** Most of these should be redone annually; say so on the page.

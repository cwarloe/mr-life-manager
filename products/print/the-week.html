Week ending Sunday:

Use the nightly routine in areas already reset. A room or pile still needing its first reset is separate work; name the next area and its finish below. Keep the areas you have finished usable while you tackle the rest.

Every night, before bed

1Empty the sinkWash or load the dishes. Leave the sink empty.
2Wipe the countersAnd the table you eat at. Leave them usable.
3Put things awayReturn loose items to their known homes.

Time your full close-out once and allow that time each night. A dish backlog or belongings without homes need reset work, not a faster lap. Check a night only when its close-out is finished.

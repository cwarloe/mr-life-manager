The Week

Mr. Life ManagerDave's system, on one page

Every night, before bed · about ten minutes

1Empty the sinkCompletely. Dishwasher or washed.
2Wipe the countersAnd the table you eat at.
3One lapPut back anything not where it lives.

This is the part that matters. A kitchen reset every night never needs rescuing. Skip it four days and you have a project.

DayThe one job, on top of the nightly ten minutes
MondayFloorsSweep, vacuum or mop. Dust what needs it — blinds, mirrors, shelves. Include the entry and stairs.
TuesdayLaundry, start to finishWash, dry, fold, and put it away the same day. Folded and left in the basket doesn't count.
WednesdayBedroom and the main bathroomFloors, surfaces, mirror, toilet, sink, tub. Check whether the windows need doing.
ThursdayThe kitchen, properlyAppliances, cabinet fronts, and clear the old food out of the fridge. Wipe the shelves while it's empty.
FridayNothing extra. Just the nightly ten.
SaturdayThe other bathroom, and the carSecond bathroom top to bottom. Sweep the garage. Clear out the car and the trunk.
SundayNothing extra. Just the nightly ten.

If you only do one thing

Empty the sink before bed.

Move jobs to whichever day suits you. But everything on the list is done by Sunday night. The order is yours. The finishing isn't.

Before any of this works, the place has to start at zero once — everything with a home, nothing left to deal with later. Then this keeps it there. mrlifemanager.com

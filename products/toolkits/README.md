# Toolkits

Bundled systems — several artifacts that solve one situation end to end.

## Why toolkits are the most on-brand format

A toolkit is **a system, not reading.** "Practical systems for everyday
adulthood" is the positioning; a toolkit is that positioning made literal.

They also carry higher perceived value than a PDF at the same production cost,
anchor bundles well, and are the format most differentiated from what everyone
else in the category sells.

## Composition

A toolkit typically contains:

1. A short guide explaining the system and why it works
2. Two to four worksheets or templates
3. A checklist
4. A calendar or cadence reference
5. A one-page quick-reference — often the piece people actually use

## Standards

- **It has to work.** A toolkit is a system, so it needs testing with real people
  before sale.
- **Complete, not padded.** Everything needed for the situation; nothing added to
  justify a price.
- **Format-independent where possible.** Notion and Sheets versions add value but
  create platform dependency and support burden. PDF first.
- **Include a worked example.** Show what "done" looks like.

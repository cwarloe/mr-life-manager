# Courses

Structured multi-module programs — text-based, cohort-based, or video.

## ⚠️ Deliberately deferred

Video courses are **explicitly not in the MVP**
([business-model.md](../../docs/business/business-model.md), Model 4). The reasons
are worth restating so this doesn't get relitigated:

- Very high production cost (scripting, filming, editing, hosting) for an
  unvalidated audience
- Notoriously low completion rates
- Aging video is expensive to re-shoot
- The same content in text sells now, at a fraction of the cost

**Trigger to revisit:** text products selling consistently.

## Preferred first format: a live cohort

A **group coaching cohort** ($299–$499, six weeks, small group) has better
economics than a video course, costs almost nothing to produce, teaches you what
people actually get stuck on, and generates testimonials. It's the right first
"course," and it makes any eventual video version dramatically better.

## Standards, when the time comes

- **Completion mechanics matter more than production value.** A course nobody
  finishes produces no outcomes and no testimonials.
- **Daily actions small enough to actually do.** Ten minutes, not an hour.
- **Every module produces an artifact** the person keeps.
- **Build for licensing:** a facilitator guide turns a course into an
  institutional product.

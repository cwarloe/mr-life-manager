# Ebooks

Multi-chapter guides. The **core of the MVP** and the revenue center of the
[product ladder](../../docs/business/product-ladder.md).

## The flagship

### ★ First Place: The Practical Guide to Running Your First Home — $49

A complete operating manual for someone running their own home for the first
time. Covers setup, room by room, cleaning, food, laundry, maintenance, money,
paperwork, time, and hospitality.

**Why this title works**
- It names a **moment**, not a topic — and the moment is the buying trigger
- It reads twice: your first place to live, *and* putting things in their place
- Broad enough to justify $49, specific enough to be one clear promise

**Why this product**
- It's the **spine of everything else.** The 30-Day Reset, the persona editions,
  and eventually a course are all repackagings of it.
- It's the **licensable asset.** Institutions buy curriculum, not tips.

**Planned editions:** student, solo, couples — same spine, adjusted framing and
examples. Roughly 20% new writing for a new market, which is the highest-ROI move
available in the entire plan.

## Standards

- **Specific over comprehensive.** Numbers, cadences, and steps beat coverage.
- **Read out of order.** Nobody reads a reference book front to back. Each
  chapter stands alone.
- **Good-enough and ideal standards**, with a clear statement of which is fine.
- **Modular**, so chapters can be extracted into other products and licensed.

## The most important production note

**Presell or sell a short version before writing the long one.**

A 40-page guide that sells beats a 200-page book that doesn't, and you find out
in a fraction of the time. The main risk in Phase 3 of the
[roadmap](../../planning/roadmap.md) is spending three months writing something
nobody wanted.

Write a `brief.md` before building — see [../README.md](../README.md).

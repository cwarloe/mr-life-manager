Three organizing checklists · Drafts

Get your space back for the things you do in it.

When finding things or using a table turns into a project, start with the problem you recognize. Each checklist ends with a useful part of daily life working again.

These are three draft approaches to try. They have not yet been tested with readers. You can check the steps here or print this page for paper copies.

Checklist 1 · Draft

Every drawer has become a junk drawer.

Get your everyday kitchen tools out of the jumble, so making dinner starts with cooking instead of searching.

Today's job: Work on the kitchen drawers you reach into to prepare and eat a normal meal. Leave the bedroom, office, and other drawers for another day. Finish one drawer before emptying the next.

Have ready: A clear patch of counter, a cloth, a trash bag, and a box for things leaving the kitchen. Small containers you already own can serve as dividers.

Your finished win

You can prepare and eat a normal meal without searching several drawers for the basics. An unrelated drawer can still be untidy.

Keep the result: Return each tool to its group after use. If a group keeps overflowing, revisit what needs daily access instead of starting a new junk drawer.

Checklist 2 · Draft

I put things away. Then I can't find them.

Make the household cupboard or hall closet a place you can find things, rather than the place things disappear.

Today's job: Work on the cupboard or closet where household odds and ends collect. Start with the things you repeatedly lose there. This is not a house-wide search or an inventory of everything you own.

Have ready: A clear working surface, a cloth, a trash bag, paper for labels, and containers you already have. Leave other people's private belongings for them to decide about.

Your finished win

You can identify what is in the cupboard and retrieve the things you use, without opening every box. Missing items have a specific next action.

Keep the result: Put things back in the same named homes. If you share the storage, show the other people the arrangement so they can use it too.

Checklist 3 · Draft

I have to clear the table before we can eat.

Get your table back for meals, and give the things that keep covering it somewhere to go.

Today's job: Work on the dining table and the chairs needed for the people who normally eat there. Keep the rest of the room outside this job. The finish is sitting down to a meal without first relocating a pile.

Have ready: A trash bag, a cleaning cloth, and a small box to carry things back to their homes. Use a scrap of paper for anything that needs a later action.

Your finished win

The people who normally eat here can sit down to a meal without clearing the table first. The objects that usually block it have named destinations.

Keep the result: After using the table for another activity, return those materials to their homes. If the same object keeps landing here, make its home easier to use.

Who Mr. Life Manager is

Dave actually knew how to do this.

Mr. Life Manager is a real person — Dave, who knew how to run a home and, over years, taught me. I'm the apprentice. I wrote it down.

When I told him people would pay to learn this, he was baffled. His answer has stuck with me ever since:

It's all obvious. I'd be embarrassed telling people things I assumed they already knew. — Dave

Which is exactly why it doesn't get handed down. The people who know this can't see it as knowledge. You've probably had the same experience: you know how to run a house, and you've had a hard time explaining it without sounding like you're nagging. That's not a failure of parenting. It's that this stuff is genuinely hard to teach from the inside.

Nothing here talks down to them, and nothing here makes them wrong for not knowing. I had to be taught all of it too.

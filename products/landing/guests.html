Mr. Life Manager

Someone's coming over and your place isn't ready.

Six steps. Welcome them into a room with clear surfaces, a clean bathroom, an empty sink, and the trash taken out.

Grab these first: trash bags, a basket or box, dish supplies, cleaner and a cloth, a toilet brush, a clean hand towel, and a broom or vacuum.

You're doing the sink, the room you'll sit in, one bathroom, and the floors between them. Nothing else.

Nothing to sign up for. Start at one.

Ready to start? Give it about an hour.

Faster if the dishwasher is empty. Longer if the sink is full.

  1. Bag the kitchen trash and let in fresh air.

    Tie off the kitchen trash, replace the bag, and set the tied bag where you will collect it for the final trip, clear of the doorway. Open a window if conditions allow.

    You will take the kitchen and bathroom bags out together at the end.

  2. Empty the sink and wipe the counter.

    Load the dishwasher if you have one and there is room. Otherwise wash the dishes and leave them on the rack. Finish the dishes in the sink, then wipe the adjoining counter. Soaking a pan may add waiting time.

    An empty sink is the finish line for this step. Handwashing a backlog needs more time; moving dirty dishes out of sight would leave it unfinished.

  3. Clear the sitting area into a basket.

    In the room you will use, collect loose belongings from tables, seats and the floor. Use another box if the first fills. Put the containers outside the guest area, clear of walkways. Leave storage decisions for tomorrow.

    You are making room to sit together. The basket holds a separate sorting job.

  4. Clean the bathroom you will offer them.

    Clean the toilet bowl and seat, sink and mirror with suitable products. Wipe the floor around the toilet. Follow label directions, including any waiting time. Put out a clean hand towel and tie off the bathroom trash.

    Finish one usable bathroom. Keep toilet-cleaning cloths separate from sink and kitchen cloths.

  5. Sweep or vacuum the route they will use.

    Do the entryway and the sitting-room floor. Collect the visible crumbs and dirt, then put the floor tool away.

    Keeping to the spaces you will use prevents this from expanding into every room.

  6. Take the trash out and set the lighting.

    Collect both tied trash bags and take them to the outdoor bin, chute or dumpster. Keep the route clear. Back inside, turn on comfortable lighting; use existing lamps if you have them.

    With the trash outside and seats clear, you can welcome your guests and sit down.

While cleaning

Do not mix cleaning products. Follow each label for safe use and ventilation. Keep the floor dry enough to walk on safely.

The place is ready for company.

The sink is empty, the surfaces are clear, the bathroom is clean, and the trash is outside. The floors where they will walk are swept. You can welcome them in and sit down together.

Sorting the basket is a separate job for tomorrow.

About that basket.

The guest area is ready; the basket still needs sorting. Pick a time tomorrow to put its contents in their homes and choose homes for anything that lacks one.

What just happened

You got the spaces you will use ready.

The task had a boundary: sink, sitting room, bathroom, walking route and trash. The other rooms can wait.

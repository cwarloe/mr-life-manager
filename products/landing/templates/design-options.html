Reset · Company

Someone's coming over and your place isn't ready.

Six steps. Welcome them into a room with clear surfaces, a clean bathroom, an empty sink, and the trash taken out.

Gather what you will use

Trash bags, a basket or box, dishwashing supplies, suitable cleaners and cloths, a toilet brush, a clean hand towel, and a broom or vacuum.

Reserve a one-hour block

This is a planning allowance, not a tested finish time.

  1. 1

    Bag the kitchen trash and let in fresh air.

    Tie off the kitchen trash, replace the bag, and set the tied bag where you will collect it for the final trip, clear of the doorway.

  2. 2

    Empty the sink and wipe the counter.

    Load the dishwasher if you have one and there is room. Otherwise wash the dishes and leave them on the rack.

  3. 3

    Clear the sitting area into a basket.

    In the room you will use, collect loose belongings from tables, seats and the floor. Leave storage decisions for tomorrow.

  4. 4

    Clean the bathroom you will offer them.

    Clean the toilet bowl and seat, sink and mirror with suitable products. Put out a clean hand towel.

  5. 5

    Sweep or vacuum the route they will use.

    Do the entryway and the sitting-room floor. Collect the visible crumbs and dirt, then put the floor tool away.

  6. 6

    Take the trash out and set the lighting.

    Collect both tied trash bags and take them out. Back inside, turn on comfortable lighting.

The place is ready for company.

The sink is empty, the surfaces are clear, the bathroom is clean, and the trash is outside. You can welcome them in and sit down together.

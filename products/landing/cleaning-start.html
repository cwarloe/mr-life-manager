Clean one kitchen work surface with a suitable product and a cloth. Start with what you have, rather than buying another shelf of cleaners.

Today's job. Choose a washable kitchen work surface. Check its care instructions and the cleaner's label; use a product approved for that surface. You'll need that product and a clean cloth. If none of your products is suitable, identify what is needed before picking a time to start.

Nothing to sign up for. Start at one.

Ready to do this? Start with the first step.

If you are not in the right place or ready to act, pick the exact time you will start. Set aside ten minutes to begin.

  1. Choose the surface you need next.

    Pick the patch where you prepare food, put down groceries, or do another everyday job. Move its loose items to their homes, or into one temporary box out of the way.

    A usable working area is a clearer finish than deciding to clean the kitchen.

  2. Read the two sets of directions.

    Check the surface's care instructions and the product label. Confirm the product is suitable, and note how to use it and whether rinsing is needed.

    The label and the surface instructions are more useful than a guess based on the bottle's color.

  3. Get one product and a clean cloth.

    Use the suitable product you identified. Keep other cleaning products out of this job; do not mix them.

    One compatible product avoids unnecessary purchases and mixing.

  4. Clean the exposed area.

    Use the product as directed, including any preparation or waiting time the label requires. Wipe with the cloth.

    Following the directions is the task. More products or harder scrubbing are not automatically better.

  5. Finish as the directions require.

    Rinse if directed, then leave the surface ready for its normal use. Put back only the items that belong there.

    A surface ready to work on is more useful than a cleared area that immediately becomes storage again.

  6. Give the supplies a home.

    Put the product away according to its storage directions and deal with the used cloth. Keep the supplies for this task in one known, suitable place.

    Next time you need this surface clean, you will know what to get and where it is.

You can use that surface.

One work area is clean. You know which product belongs with this job. The whole kitchen is not today's assignment.

This job is finished.

Keep the start easy.

Give the product and cloth a suitable storage place near where you use them. Follow the storage directions on the product label.

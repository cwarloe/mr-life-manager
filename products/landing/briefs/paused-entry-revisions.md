# Paused entry revisions after the completion audit

These revisions supersede the older briefs for the seven paused entry drafts.
They implement the [paused completion audit](../../../docs/research/paused-checklist-completion-audit.md)
and preserve the six-step task structure. All seven remain unpublished; their
public URLs continue to redirect to the five active choices. No signup,
publication flag, shared asset, active entry page or email sequence was changed.

The six newer entries retain their existing ten- or fifteen-minute calendar
blocks explicitly as time to start, not researched finish times. Office uses
an editorial 180-minute planning block instead of claiming a desk can reliably
be finished in two hours. Its actual work depends on volume, permissions and
records handling. These scheduling choices still require real-user testing.

## Revision decisions

- **Laundry:** one complete named basket, one person's clothes, checked storage
  capacity and hangers before spreading it out. No hidden drawer redesign.
  Missing storage keeps the task open; the basket is finished only when clothes
  have homes and the outfit and sorting surface are ready.
- **Bean wraps:** a one-person starting quantity and complete tool list before
  heating. Ready-cooked canned beans, concrete draining/assembly instructions,
  label-directed preparation/storage, appliance off. Extra eaters require
  quantities chosen first. Shopping remains a separate prerequisite, not dinner.
- **Cleaning:** identify the surface and compatible method before application;
  use product and surface directions for preparation, waiting and rinsing.
  Unknown suitability requires an answer and rescheduling, not a guessed
  generic cleaner or a false cleaning-complete state.
- **Sitting spot:** choose an actual activity and test at the intended time of
  day. Existing equipment and safe movable arrangements only. A recorded
  missing-light/furniture need is useful but does not count as a working spot.
- **Kitchen close:** name the people, breakfast, dish set, work patch and needed
  appliance before work. The full set and access route must be usable; hidden
  dirty required dishes or missing ingredients keep the task open.
- **Shared dishes:** mutual willingness and acceptance throughout. No
  unilateral ownership or deadline. The success link explicitly requires both
  people's acceptance of the complete agreement. An unresolved discussion
  remains unresolved, with a mutually chosen follow-up if desired.
- **Office:** records and ownership rules before touching papers, manageable
  staging instead of a floor dump, no five-second/title-only disposal or
  immediate-action detours. Existing labeled storage and secure review areas.
  Actual returns or approved controlled holding with named recipient, location
  and follow-up; pending receipt remains pending. The outcome is a usable desk
  with controlled open work, not a claim every records decision was solved.

Completion links and instructions describe physical/agreed outcomes. The UI
cannot observe them: clicking through six steps is not evidence they happened.
The source explicitly directs blocked users to resolve the prerequisite or
reschedule rather than mark Finished.

## Verification and remaining work

Source HTML and internal links pass repository validation. A local browser
harness serves retained source pages directly while built public paths remain
redirects. It checks six-step navigation at 320/375px, Why-expanded navigation,
completion-link routing, calendar duration in ICS and Google links, resume, and
one-page printing with embedded fonts. Print scales are retuned after editing.
A stub completion destination in that source harness tests link wiring only;
it does not publish or validate the currently paused generated completion pages.

Publication readiness still requires representative full-task sessions,
starting-condition records, elapsed time, observed results and perceived load.
Recipe acceptance/portion needs, surface identification, other people's
agreement, records authority and equipment access need testing with the actual
audience. The source edits improve the design; they do not establish completion
rates or give a basis to reactivate the drafts automatically.

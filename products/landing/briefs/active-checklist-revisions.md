# Active checklist revisions after the completion audit

These revisions supersede the older active-entry briefs where they conflict.
They apply to first-night, guests, dinner, underwater and one-room only. Other
checklists remain paused.

The [completion audit](../../../docs/research/checklist-completion-audit.md)
is a research-informed design review, not observed user validation. The full
six-step outcomes remain the job; doing only the first steps is not a completed
checklist. No sources were changed and no email sequence was changed.

## Timing

Remove per-step minute badges and untested finish-time promises. Offer explicit
calendar planning blocks instead: first-night 90 minutes, guests 60, underwater
90, one-room 180. Dinner keeps ten minutes to start and makes no finish-time
promise. These are editorial scheduling allowances with more room than the
old totals, not empirically calibrated estimates or cognitive-science findings.
Each page explains the work can take longer. Setup, missing supplies, dish/mail
backlogs and donation travel are stated before Start rather than hidden inside
the task. Actual completion times still require representative household tests.

## Decisions

- First-night: list essentials upfront; remove retrospective car-packing advice
  and deposit guarantees. Include a fault-report example. Record blocked or
  unsafe checks explicitly; requesting access does not mean a check or repair
  is complete. Controls still need a follow-up until located.
- Guests: list tools and full physical scope upfront. Keep sink-empty outcome
  for both dishwasher and handwashing, with extra time for a backlog. Follow
  product labels and allow waiting time. Remove guest-judgment pressure. Keep
  trash outside as part of the full finish.
- Dinner: allow another available container, safe carrying weight, and only one
  known-home return trip. Storage decisions remain separate from eating.
- Underwater: keep kitchen, main room, bed, dirty clothes, mail and next action.
  State the backlog affects time. No arbitrary 8 PM machine rule, bill-payment
  cutoff or zero-effort claim. Open mail, retain documents, record due dates and
  urgent/unclear action rather than trying to solve every bill while cleaning.
- One-room: define a complete usable area, such as workbench plus floor,
  storage unit plus floor, or reading corner with seat, surface and path.
  Confirm disposal and donation handoff before spreading things out; collection
  is an alternative to car transport. The job includes the actual handoff,
  not merely loading a car. Replace speed-based disposal and fixed sprints with
  deliberate categories and optional breaks. Protect other people's things,
  records and intentionally kept seasonal/emergency/sentimental items.
  Give any consent or record question a labeled location and a specific next
  action rather than an anonymous unsorted pile. Existing labeled containers
  work; buying clear bins is not required.

## Verification

The source and built-site checks preserve the active five, email wiring,
calendar choice and paused routes. Browser checks verify six-step navigation,
completion, calendar duration, resume and a single printed page. Print scales
were retuned with embedded brand fonts after the edits. These checks verify
software and layout, not whether a person achieves a household outcome.

## Audience, domains, and outcomes

For Maya or Tyler learning independent living, Dana trying to reduce household
friction, and couples or roommates sharing daily jobs
([audiences](../../../docs/vision/target-audiences.md)). Each maps to an existing
[problem](../../../docs/research/customer-problems.md): laundry piles, food
indecision, cleaning-product confusion, rooms without a useful setup, recurring
kitchen backlog, or unspoken job ownership.

Primary domain is Home; food also covers Food, and the dishes agreement covers
Relationships ([domain map](../../../frameworks/life-domains.md)).
Target [outcomes](../../../docs/vision/customer-outcomes.md) are functional homes,
less decision fatigue, and better routines. The claims describe today's
observable finish, not lasting behavioral change or a completely fair household.

## Method and source boundaries

Apply [Dave's principles engine](../../../frameworks/dave-principles.md):
decide a bounded goal; prepare supplies and access; reset only that scope;
give things visible, useful homes; leave the next use easier.
House principles 1, 4, 5, 8, 9, and 10 govern the writing
([principles](../../../docs/vision/principles.md)).

- Laundry: use the existing guide's laundry-chair section, grouping, hanging,
  and one-load-at-a-time close-out. Do not turn it into a complete wash cycle.
- Food: use the existing canned-bean taco pattern. State ingredients before
  starting; no raw-meat handling, temperature claims, or recipe-time promise.
  This is our food content, not a method recorded from Dave.
- Cleaning: use suitable existing products, product/surface instructions, one
  product at a time, and storage near use. Do not repeat broad claims that a
  cleaner works on every surface or require buying the full supply list.
- Sitting spot: use existing lamps and daylight, a clear seat, and a small
  useful surface. No fixture installation, bulb compatibility, or universal
  claim that lighting is the explanation for discomfort.
- Kitchen close: reduce the daily rows to a usable breakfast area. If there is
  a larger backlog, the existing underwater entrance remains available.
  No new food-storage durations or appliance-maintenance facts are introduced.
- Dishes agreement: use one owner, noticing and supplies as part of ownership,
  a shared minimum, and a dated check-back from the existing worksheet.
  Another person's agreement is required; assigning them a chore is not a win.

The household and food guides are existing authored material, not missing Dave
sources. Instructions to follow product or package labels are retained where
appropriate rather than adding technical reference material.

## Format, delivery, and price

Six actions per page, copied from the working dinner/guests entry shell:
reading mode, one-step doing mode, printable Letter sheet, and an explicit
completion page. Every new page opts into the shared deferred-start calendar
flow, including current-step resume and the existing move cap.

Calendar blocks (10 or 15 minutes) reserve time to start. They are not tested
completion estimates; cooking time depends on the ingredients and package
directions. The dishes page asks both people to be available; an agreement is
not counted as completed if they do not reach one.

Free emergency/small-task entrances under ADR-020 and ADR-022 in the
[decision log](../../../planning/decisions.md). All new routes use
`email_signup=False`, no next-step offer, and no new MailerLite promise.
The longer material can support later levels, but this change sets no tier
pricing or offer. Existing email-collecting routes and their sequences remain.

The homepage groups choices by present need so this expansion does not become
one undifferentiated list. Office remains direct-link only.

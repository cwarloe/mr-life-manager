# Entry brief — Dinner's ready. There's nowhere to eat.

**Status:** Six-step entry point, 2026-10-27. Headline accepted by Charles;
reader completion and timing remain untested. No completion-time estimate.

## Who, problem, and outcome

For Dana, the overwhelmed householder, and Maya setting up a shared home
([audiences](../../../docs/vision/target-audiences.md)). The
[home problem](../../../docs/research/customer-problems.md#home-general) is a
table that serves as storage instead of a working eating space. The desired
[outcome](../../../docs/vision/customer-outcomes.md#cleaner-more-functional-homes)
is an ordinary meal with usable places to sit. No whole-home reset is promised.

Primary domain: Home; secondary: Hospitality
([domains](../../../frameworks/life-domains.md)). Uses
[principles](../../../docs/vision/principles.md) 5 (easy action), 8 (dignity),
9 (small enough to keep), and 10 (explain why).

## The six actions

1. Obvious trash out of the way.
2. Dishes to the kitchen; washing is a later job.
3. Return items that already have an obvious home.
4. Put unresolved items in one accessible basket off the table, seats, and walkway.
5. Wipe the eating surface.
6. Clear the seats, set the places, and sit down.

The basket is explicitly unfinished. The win is eating tonight, not solving
storage. The [eight-step table checklist](../organizing-ideas.html#clear-the-table)
is the optional next level when the reader has time to assign real homes.
The [three eight-step drafts](../../checklists/reset-your-space/versions/brief.md)
and [twelve-step guide](../../checklists/reset-your-space/checklist.md) remain intact.

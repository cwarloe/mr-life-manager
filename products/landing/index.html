Who Mr. Life Manager is

Dave actually knew how to do this.

Mr. Life Manager is a real person — Dave Webster, who knew how to run a home and, over years, taught me. I'm the apprentice. I wrote it down.

When I told him people would pay to learn this, he was baffled. His answer has stuck with me ever since:

It's all obvious. I'd be embarrassed telling people things I assumed they already knew. — Dave

Which is exactly why nobody ever told you. The people who know this can't see it as knowledge. From the inside it just looks like common sense — so it never gets handed down. Not out of indifference. Out of invisibility.

That's the whole reason this exists. And it's why nothing here talks down to you: I had to be taught all of it too.

Where this goes

Order isn't the point.

A tidy house nobody enters is a museum. The reason to get a handle on all this is so you can open the door — have people over on two hours' notice, feed someone, put a friend up for the night, without a two-day panic first.

Systems buy you margin. Margin is what lets you say come over. That's the whole point, and everything else is just the machinery that makes it possible.

Who Mr. Life Manager is

Dave actually knew how to do this.

Mr. Life Manager is a real person — Dave, who knew how to run a home and, over years, taught me. I'm the apprentice. I wrote it down.

When I told him people would pay to learn this, he was baffled. His answer has stuck with me ever since:

It's all obvious. I'd be embarrassed telling people things I assumed they already knew. — Dave

Which is exactly why nobody ever told you. The people who know this can't see it as knowledge. From the inside it just looks like common sense — so it never gets handed down. Not because they don't care, but because they don't notice they know it.

That's the whole reason this exists. And it's why nothing here talks down to you: I had to be taught all of it too.

The promise

A working home, one decision at a time.

The broader problem touches food, time, maintenance, paperwork, and relationships. You do not need all of that today. You need the next useful action and somewhere reliable for the recurring work to live.

That is where this starts: visible household problems, handled in an order that prevents the next crisis instead of creating another list to manage.

Where this goes

Order isn't the point.

A tidy house matters because it lets people in. The reason to keep up with all this is so you can open the door — have people over on two hours' notice, feed someone, put a friend up for the night, without two days of rushing first.

A few daily habits give you spare time and energy, and that is what lets you say come over. That's the whole point, and everything else on this site is how you get there.

Nobody expected you to have known this.

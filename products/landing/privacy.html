Privacy, in one screen.

You can use every page here without giving us an email. If you leave one at the bottom of a page, we use it for one thing: the printable of that page, then two short follow-ups that get smaller and then stop.

  • Right away: a one-page printable of the steps you just used
  • A few days later: a smaller version of the same job
  • About a week later: one question you can answer in a word, then nothing else on a timer

Which emails you get depends on which page you signed up from — first night, or someone coming over. We do not send a pack of every guide. We do not branch on whether you opened the last email. We do not run a win-back campaign if you go quiet.

You can unsubscribe anytime from any email. We don't sell the list. We don't share it for ads. Replies go to a person at hello@mrlifemanager.com.

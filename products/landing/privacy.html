Privacy, in one screen.

You can use every page here without giving us an email. The immediate task is already on the page, including a print option. If you leave an address afterward, we use it for one short sequence connected to the page you used.

  • Right away: one link to The Week, Dave's weekly system on one printable page
  • A few days later: one smaller action connected to where you started
  • About a week later: one question you can answer in a word, then nothing else on a timer

Which emails you get depends on which page you signed up from: first night, someone coming over, the whole house needing work, a room that became storage, or a desk you can't see anymore. We do not send a pack of every guide. We do not branch on whether you opened the last email. We do not run a win-back campaign if you go quiet.

MailerLite stores addresses submitted through the free entry pages and sends those sequences for us. You can unsubscribe anytime from any automated email or ask us to delete your address. We don't sell the list or share it for advertising. Answers to the feedback form are collected through Formspree. We use them to decide what to build next, and if you leave an email address there, we use it only to ask you one follow-up question. Replies go to a person at hello@mrlifemanager.com.

Pages with an email box load MailerLite's sign-up form as soon as they open, before you sign up. Your browser contacts MailerLite to show it, and MailerLite keeps an ID in your browser and can set cookies, one of which lasts 90 days. If you arrive from a link in one of our emails, it also tells MailerLite which email and link you clicked, so that visit is tied to your address. The site's fonts load from this site, not from Google.

We count visits and a few anonymous actions with Umami, a privacy-first analytics service: which page you used, the tag in the link if you came from a QR code, and which button you chose (start now, pick a time, finished). Umami sets no cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites, and records nothing that identifies you. The time you pick for a reminder stays in your own calendar; this site doesn't keep it.

Questions about this: same address.

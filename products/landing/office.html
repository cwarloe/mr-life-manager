You can't see your desk anymore.

Six steps to a usable desk: current tools within reach, records protected, returns handed off or controlled, and a clear working surface.

Pick one desk surface. Before you throw any paper away, know your workplace's rules for what can go and where confidential papers go.

You need boxes or trays you already have, labels, a cloth, and a secure spot for papers you need to ask someone about. Keep the walkway clear.

Nothing to sign up for. Start at one.

Ready to start? Give it about three hours.

Longer if there's a lot of paper. Know where confidential papers go before you start.

  1. Confirm handling rules and destinations.

    Identify what you may handle, retain, recycle or put in approved confidential disposal. Confirm recipients or authorized return locations. If rules are unclear, protect the material and ask its owner or records contact before discarding it. Close unrelated apps.

    A missing permission or locked records area is a real constraint. Resolve it or prepare an authorized holding location before sorting.

  2. Sort one manageable section at a time.

    Work across the desk without emptying everything onto the floor. Separate keep here, return or archive, approved discard, and needs review. Keep confidential material in authorized storage and paths clear.

    Limited staging keeps the whole desk restorable if you are interrupted.

  3. Record decisions and follow-up without starting new jobs.

    Read enough to classify each item under the rules you checked. Keep uncertain records and other people's property. Label review items with the owner or question and a next action. Write tasks on an action list instead of answering messages or doing each paper's job now.

    There is no five-second disposal deadline. Finding a task and completing that task are different work.

  4. Complete returns or establish a controlled handoff.

    Take approved waste to the correct destination. Hand return items to their recipient or approved location. If the recipient is unavailable, use an authorized secure holding place and record the recipient, location and follow-up time.

    An item awaiting acceptance remains pending. A controlled holding location clears the working desk without pretending the handoff happened.

  5. Put everyday tools and records in labeled homes.

    Keep current tools within comfortable reach and heavy items low. Put retained records in their authorized homes. Use existing boxes or trays with readable labels. Keep review material separate with its recorded next action.

    Clear containers are optional. A readable location and appropriate handling matter more than buying storage.

  6. Wipe the desk and set an incoming place.

    Wipe the cleared working area with a suitable method. Use an existing tray or labeled section for incoming material, subject to handling rules. Test finding the tools for your next task and check all pending actions have a location and follow-up.

    The finished desk is usable. Pending reviews and handoffs are recorded work, not erased obligations.

Keep protected material protected

Follow workplace records, privacy and disposal rules. Keep uncertain papers for authorized review; do not infer disposal permission from a digital copy. Send electronics and storage devices through approved IT or facilities handling.

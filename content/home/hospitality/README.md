# Hospitality

Opening your home and life to other people.

## Why this has its own directory

Hospitality is the **destination of the entire curriculum**, not a nice extra.
[`principles.md`](../../../docs/vision/principles.md) §7 makes the claim: order
isn't the point — order makes room for people. A tidy home nobody enters is a
museum.

This is also the brand's clearest differentiator. Every other organizing and
cleaning brand ends at *clean*. We end at *open*.

## Guiding ideas

- **Most people don't host because it feels like a two-day preparation sprint.**
  A home kept near baseline can absorb guests on short notice. Systems produce
  margin; margin produces welcome.
- **Panic-cleaning before guests trains you to avoid hosting.** Fix the baseline,
  not the panic.
- **Repeatability is the unlock.** One menu you've made six times removes almost
  all the anxiety.
- **Nobody remembers whether your baseboards were clean.** Say so plainly.

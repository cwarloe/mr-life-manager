# Cleaning

Routines, supplies, standards, and cadences.

**Positioning note:** cleaning is the **entry point, not the brand**
([ADR-005](../../../planning/decisions.md)). It's the most visible symptom, so
it's how people find us — and it's the most crowded category with the weakest
economics. Every piece of cleaning content should bridge to the larger system.

## Planned content

**P1 (see [backlog.md](../../../planning/backlog.md))**
- How often should I clean what? — the cadence reference
- Cleaning supply starter list — what to buy, what to skip, what it costs
- The nightly kitchen reset (10 minutes)
- The weekly reset routine
- The 20-minute emergency reset

**P2**
- Cleaning routines organized by frequency, not by room
- Bathroom: the weekly system
- What actually cleans what (and what never to mix)
- The right order to clean a room, and why it saves time
- The things that don't look dirty: dishwasher filter, washing machine, dryer
  vent, fridge coils, showerhead, disposal
- Roommate standards and chore agreements
- Deep-clean checklists by season

## Guiding ideas

- **Nobody knows the cadence.** "How often should I…" is the most-asked question
  in this domain. Answer it plainly and completely.
- **Cleaning is only overwhelming because it's deferred.** Teach the reset rhythm
  before teaching technique.
- **Give the good-enough standard and the ideal**, and say which is fine.
- **Never moralize about mess.** ([principles.md](../../../docs/vision/principles.md) §8)

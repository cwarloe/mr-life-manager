## Guiding ideas

- **Disorder is unmade decisions, not dirt.** Fix the decision, not the symptom.
- **Watch where things actually land.** That's data about where their home should
  be — relocate the home to match the behavior, don't fight it.
- **The container trap:** buying organizing products before deciding what goes
  where is the most common and most expensive mistake in this domain. Assign
  homes first, buy only what's still missing.
- **Not minimalism.** We're agnostic about how much you own. Full and functional
  is a success. ([brand-positioning.md](../../../docs/business/brand-positioning.md))

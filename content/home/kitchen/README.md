## Guiding ideas

- **The kitchen is the keystone room.** Get it working and the rest of the house
  follows; leave it broken and everything else gets harder.
- **The compounding loop:** a dirty kitchen makes cooking harder → you order out
  → money leaves and the kitchen stays dirty. Break it at the cheapest point:
  the ten minutes after dinner.
- **Repertoire beats recipes.** Six meals you can make without thinking is worth
  more than a hundred you'll never try.
- **Food waste is real money** leaving the house weekly, usually unnoticed.

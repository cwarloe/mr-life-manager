## Guiding ideas

- **Unassigned responsibility gets absorbed by whoever notices first.** That
  person becomes the household's memory, and burns out. Assign explicitly.
  ([everything-has-a-place.md](../../frameworks/everything-has-a-place.md) §6)
- **Small unaddressed hurts accumulate exactly like clutter.** A twenty-minute
  awkward conversation now prevents a two-year silence later — and the
  conversation only gets harder with time.
- **Can't host → relationships stay shallow.** This is the loop that ties the
  whole business together.
- **Caveat, and it matters:** relationships are not machines. This framework
  should never make them transactional. The claim is narrow — *attention given
  regularly costs less than repair* — not that people can be maintained on a
  schedule.

## Distinction from Hospitality

[`content/home/hospitality/`](../home/hospitality/) is the *practice* of opening
your home. This directory is the *fabric* of the relationships themselves.

## Guiding ideas

- **"Do it now" means decide now, do later on purpose.** Not everything
  immediately — that's just being an interrupt handler.
- **The cost of a task is the carrying, not the doing.** Deciding ends the
  carrying.
- **Margin is prevention.** Slack is what keeps one delay from cascading through
  a week.
- **The weekly review is the keystone.** Every other technique here decays
  without it.

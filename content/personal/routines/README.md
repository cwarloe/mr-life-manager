# Routines

Daily, weekly, and seasonal rhythms — the machinery that carries behavior when
motivation doesn't.

## Planned content

**P1**
- The nightly closing shift (10 minutes)
- The weekly reset (45–90 minutes)
- The morning that actually works

**P2**
- The monthly reset
- Seasonal resets, four times a year
- Building a routine that survives a bad week
- Recovering after a routine collapses — **the skill that determines whether any
  of this lasts**
- Routines for couples and roommates
- Routines when capacity is reduced (illness, new baby, grief, travel)

## Guiding ideas

- **Systems beat memory and motivation.**
  ([principles.md](../../../docs/vision/principles.md) §4) The test for any
  routine: *would this still happen on your worst week?*
- **Reset before overwhelm.** §6. Disorder compounds; a 20%-off room takes ten
  minutes, an 80%-off room takes a Saturday and gets postponed instead.
- **Small enough to keep.** §9. A ten-minute routine you do beats a
  forty-five-minute one you abandon.
- **Regression is normal.** Every routine lapses eventually. Teach the recovery
  move, not just the routine.

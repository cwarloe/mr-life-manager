Paperwork, documents, renewals, and the vehicle — the obligations that prove and
enable your life.

([`life-domains.md`](../../../frameworks/life-domains.md)).

## Guiding ideas

- **This is the highest-stakes application of "everything has a place."** Most
  people can absorb a messy closet. Nobody absorbs losing a birth certificate the
  week they need it.
- **A stack of mail is a stack of unmade decisions with deadlines attached.**
- **Someone else should be able to find these.** In an emergency, a spouse,
  parent, or executor needs to locate your documents without you.
- **Know retention periods** so you can throw things away with confidence.
  Keeping everything forever fails the same way as keeping nothing.

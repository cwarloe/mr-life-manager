# Personal

The systems that run *you* rather than the house — time, attention, information,
and the paperwork that proves your life exists.

| Subdirectory | Covers |
|---|---|
| [`routines/`](routines/) | Daily, weekly, and seasonal rhythms |
| [`time-management/`](time-management/) | Calendar, tasks, planning, review |
| [`digital-life/`](digital-life/) | Files, email, passwords, backups, photos |
| [`personal-admin/`](personal-admin/) | Documents, renewals, vehicles, admin |

**Also lands here for now:** transportation and vehicle admin, in
`personal-admin/`. It's a separate domain in
[`life-domains.md`](../../frameworks/life-domains.md) and can be split out later.

**Common thread:** every topic here is about **getting things out of your head
and into something reliable.** That's the same claim
[`everything-has-a-place.md`](../../frameworks/everything-has-a-place.md) makes
about objects, applied to time, information, and obligations.

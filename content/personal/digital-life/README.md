## Guiding ideas

- **Digital clutter creates the same cognitive load as physical clutter** — and
  it's worse, because it's invisible and never triggers the discomfort that
  prompts a cleanup.
- **Digital crises are unusually severe** because they're often total and
  irreversible. A flood ruins replaceable things; a dead drive with no backup
  destroys what no longer exists anywhere. And there's no visible warning.
- **Verify the backup.** It's the step everyone skips, and the one that makes the
  difference.
- **If it isn't backed up, it doesn't have a place.**

# Work

Managing the job — not doing the job.

## Scope boundary

We cover the **systems around work**: tracking commitments, managing email,
boundaries, benefits, career admin. We do **not** cover the craft of any
profession. That's permanently out of scope
([life-domains.md](../../frameworks/life-domains.md)).

## Planned content

**P2**
- A task system that catches everything
- Email is not a to-do list — when it is, other people set your priorities
- Preparing for and running a meeting
- Boundaries between work and home, especially remote
- Tracking accomplishments so reviews and résumés don't start from zero
- Understanding your benefits and PTO — and actually taking it
- Professional documents: offers, reviews, certifications
- Career admin: résumé, LinkedIn, references, maintained before you need them
- Workspace setup
- Commute and remote-work rhythms

## Guiding ideas

- **Career admin done in a crisis is done badly.** Maintain it on a cadence like
  anything else. ([preventative-vs-crisis.md](../../frameworks/preventative-vs-crisis.md))
- **This is the domain most adjacent to productivity culture**, which we're
  positioned against. Keep it practical and mechanical — no optimization
  ideology, no implication that the reader should produce more.
  ([brand-positioning.md](../../docs/business/brand-positioning.md))
- Work systems and personal systems should be **one system, or cleanly two** —
  never one and a half.
